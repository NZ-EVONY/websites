How Words With Friends scoring differs

The game looks like Scrabble but prices letters differently, so the best play for the same rack often changes. Notable differences:

LetterScrabbleWords With Friends
B, C, M, P34
H43
J810
L, N, U12
V45
Y43

Playing all seven tiles earns 35 points here, not 50, so a big-tile play on a premium square often beats a bingo.

Tips

  • Two-letter plays with J, Q, X and Z across double- or triple-letter squares score in both directions.
  • The board's triple-letter squares sit closer to the centre than in Scrabble, so high-value tiles pay off earlier.

Words With Friends uses its own dictionary. Our list is broad English and may include a few words the game rejects, or miss newer slang it accepts.
