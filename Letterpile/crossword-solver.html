Writing a pattern

One character per square. Known letters as themselves; unknown squares as ?, _ or .. The pattern's length is the answer's length.

PatternFinds
?A?Efour letters, A second, E last: BAKE, CAFE, GAME…

Q???? + exclude Ufive-letter Q words without a U, like QORMA

Using the extra filters

Must include is for letters you know are in the answer but not where, from a crossing clue you half-remember. Exclude removes letters from the empty squares only, so a letter you've already placed is never excluded.

Answers with spaces, like phrases, aren't supported. Enter the letters without the gap.

Tip

Crossing answers are your best friend. Fill in every letter you're sure of from crossing words before searching. Two known letters usually cut thousands of options to a handful.
