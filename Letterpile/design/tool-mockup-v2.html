Sort results

This finder is for crossword-style tile games where you hold a rack of up to seven letters. Type your rack, with ? for a blank, and it lists every word your tiles can make, highest score first, using the letter values commonly used in Scrabble-style games. Scores are base tile values: the finder can't see your board's bonus squares, so use the list to spot options and the board to choose between them.

How to use it

  1. Type your rack in Your rack, up to 7 letters. Use ? for each blank tile.
  2. Optionally narrow by start, end, contents or length under Advanced filters.
  3. Press Find words. Switch the view between “Highest score first” and “Grouped by length”.
  4. Tap a word for its points and an optional definition.

Reading the results

Each chip shows the word and its base score. The coloured edge shows its length: 7 letters coral, 6 sunshine, 5 mint, 4 blue and 3 violet. Words that use all seven tiles are filled in coral.

Frequently asked questions

How do blank tiles work?

Type a ? for each blank. A blank can stand for any letter. The tool uses your real letters first.

Why is a word I expected missing?

Each game uses its own dictionary. This tool uses the open ENABLE word list.
