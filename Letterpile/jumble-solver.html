Jumble Solver

Enter each scrambled word from your puzzle, one per box. Every one is solved at once, then the circled letters can be worked on for the final answer.

Solving a jumble

  1. Type each scrambled word into its own box. Order doesn't matter.
  2. Press Solve all. Most jumble words have exactly one answer; where there are several, pick the one whose circled letters make sense.
  3. Collect the circled letters into Final puzzle letters for single-word and two-word answers to the cartoon's punchline. Longer punchlines usually need some human judgement, but the pairs are a strong head start.

If a box shows no answer, check for a mistyped letter. Newspaper jumbles only use common words, so the answer is almost always the most everyday word in the list.

Tip

Stuck on the punchline? Try pulling the most likely short word, like THE or AN, out of the final letters first, then solve what's left.
