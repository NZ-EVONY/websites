Word Combiner

Feed it two to four words and it splices them together every sensible way. Blends that turn out to be real words float to the top.

How blends are built

For each pair of words, the combiner takes the start of one and the end of the other: breakfast + lunch gives brunch, smoke + fog gives smog. It keeps at least a fifth of each word, so both sources are still recognisable, and it drops anything that has no vowel or stacks up four consonants.

Results come in three piles:

  • Blends that are already words: splices that land on a real dictionary word, sometimes the famous one.
  • New blends: invented words, most balanced first. This is where names come from.
  • Joined: the words simply stuck together in every order.

Ideas

Business and product names, pet names, usernames, couple names, fantasy creatures, and puns for group chats. Try your two names, or two things your product does.

Tip

Short, punchy blends usually come from pairing a word's first syllable with the other word's last one. Check the top of New blends.
