STRONGLY

Last updated: October 17, 2026

STRONGLY is an 8-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has one vowel and seven consonants, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with S and ends with Y; spelled backward it reads YLGNORTS.
