BIT

Last updated: October 17, 2026

BIT is a 3-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has one vowel and two consonants, and one syllable when spoken. It starts with B and ends with T; spelled backward it reads TIB.

Points

BIT scores 5 with common tile values (B is worth 3) and 6 with Words With Friends values, or 10 and 12 on a double word square.

Anagrams of BIT

No other word in the list uses exactly the letters B-I-T, so BIT has no anagrams.

Words made from the letters of BIT

No shorter word of three or more letters can be made from the letters of BIT.
