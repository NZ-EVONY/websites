MISTAKES

Last updated: October 17, 2026

MISTAKES is an 8-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has three vowels and five consonants, with S twice, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with M and ends with S; spelled backward it reads SEKATSIM.
