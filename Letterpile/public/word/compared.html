COMPARED

Last updated: October 17, 2026

COMPARED is an 8-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has three vowels and five consonants, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with C and ends with D; spelled backward it reads DERAPMOC.
