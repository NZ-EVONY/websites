PLACES

Last updated: October 22, 2026

PLACES is a 6-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has two vowels and four consonants, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with P and ends with S; spelled backward it reads SECALP.

Points

PLACES scores 10 with common tile values (P is worth 3) and 13 with an alternate set of values (as used by some word apps), or 20 and 26 on a double word square.

Anagrams of PLACES

No other word in the list uses exactly the letters A-C-E-L-P-S, so PLACES has no anagrams.
