SPRING

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Meaning of SPRING

noun, verb. The season after winter, a coiled piece of metal that returns to shape, or a place where water flows from the ground; as a verb, to jump suddenly.

"In spring, the lambs arrive on the farm."

About the word

In NZ, spring runs from September to November, the opposite of the Northern Hemisphere. The verb has irregular past forms: "sprang" (it sprang open) and "sprung" (it has sprung a leak). In five-letter-plus puzzles, SPR is a common opening cluster worth testing.

SPRING is a 6-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has one vowel and five consonants, and one syllable when spoken. It starts with S and ends with G; spelled backward it reads GNIRPS.
