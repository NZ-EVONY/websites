GRINDS

Last updated: October 17, 2026

GRINDS is a 6-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has one vowel and five consonants, and one syllable when spoken. It starts with G and ends with S; spelled backward it reads SDNIRG.

Points

GRINDS scores 8 with common tile values (G is worth 2) and 10 with Words With Friends values, or 16 and 20 on a double word square.

Anagrams of GRINDS

No other word in the list uses exactly the letters D-G-I-N-R-S, so GRINDS has no anagrams.

Using some of the letters of GRINDS, each no more often than it appears, gives 37 shorter words of three or more letters, the longest being GRIND.
