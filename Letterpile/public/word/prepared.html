PREPARED

Last updated: October 30, 2026

PREPARED is an 8-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has three vowels and five consonants, with P twice and R twice and E twice, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with P and ends with D; spelled backward it reads DERAPERP.

PREPARED scores 13 with common tile values (P is worth 3) and 15 with an alternate set of values (as used by some word apps), or 26 and 30 on a double word square.
