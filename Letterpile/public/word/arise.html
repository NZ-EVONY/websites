ARISE

Last updated: October 17, 2026

ARISE is a 5-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has three vowels and two consonants, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with A and ends with E; spelled backward it reads ESIRA.

Points

ARISE scores 5 with common tile values (A is worth 1) and 5 with Words With Friends values, or 10 and 10 on a double word square.
