IDEAL

Last updated: October 30, 2026

IDEAL is a 5-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has three vowels and two consonants, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with I and ends with L; spelled backward it reads LAEDI.

Points

IDEAL scores 6 with common tile values (D is worth 2) and 7 with an alternate set of values (as used by some word apps), or 12 and 14 on a double word square.
