FRIEND

Last updated: October 17, 2026

FRIEND is a 6-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has two vowels and four consonants, and one syllable when spoken. It starts with F and ends with D; spelled backward it reads DNEIRF.

FRIEND scores 10 with common tile values (F is worth 4) and 11 with Words With Friends values, or 20 and 22 on a double word square.
