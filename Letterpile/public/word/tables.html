TABLES

Last updated: October 30, 2026

TABLES is a 6-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has two vowels and four consonants, and two syllables when spoken. It starts with T and ends with S; spelled backward it reads SELBAT.

Points

TABLES scores 8 with common tile values (B is worth 3) and 10 with an alternate set of values (as used by some word apps), or 16 and 20 on a double word square.
