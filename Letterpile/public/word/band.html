BAND

Last updated: October 20, 2026

BAND is a 4-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has one vowel and three consonants, and one syllable when spoken. It starts with B and ends with D; spelled backward it reads DNAB.

Points

BAND scores 7 with common tile values (B is worth 3) and 9 with an alternate set of values (as used by some word apps), or 14 and 18 on a double word square.

Anagrams of BAND

No other word in the list uses exactly the letters A-B-D-N, so BAND has no anagrams.

Using some of the letters of BAND, each no more often than it appears, gives five shorter words of three or more letters, the longest being AND.
