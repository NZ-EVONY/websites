LACK

Last updated: October 17, 2026

LACK is a 4-letter word in the ENABLE word list, and one of the most common words in English. It has one vowel and three consonants, and one syllable when spoken. It starts with L and ends with K; spelled backward it reads KCAL.

LACK scores 10 with common tile values (K is worth 5) and 12 with Words With Friends values, or 20 and 24 on a double word square.
