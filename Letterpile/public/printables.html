Printable puzzles

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Puzzle

Your puzzle will appear here.

Make a fresh puzzle, check it on screen, and download it as a PDF: the puzzle on the first page and the answer key on the second, ready for a printer, a classroom or a long car trip. Every puzzle is new each time you press Make puzzle, built in your browser by the same code that makes our Daily Word Search and Daily Sudoku, and checked the same way before you see it.

Word searches

Choose from 45 themes and three age levels. Kids puzzles use a 10 × 10 grid, short single words and only three directions: across, down and diagonally down. Teens puzzles are 15 × 15 with six directions, including some backward words. Adults puzzles are 20 × 20 with words in all eight directions, like the daily game. Every word appears exactly once, so there's never an argument about which one counts.

Sudoku

Easy puzzles can be finished by filling in squares where only one digit fits. Medium puzzles need a little more thought about where a digit can go in a box, row or column, and hard ones need pairs and triples of candidates. Each puzzle has exactly one solution, checked by a solver before it's shown.

For teachers and parents

Rude words are always kept out: they're never in the word lists, and the random letters that fill a word search are checked so none can be read in any direction. There's no setting to turn this off. The PDFs carry a small letterpile.app credit at the foot of the page and nothing else, and nothing you make is sent anywhere: the puzzle and the PDF are created on your own device.
