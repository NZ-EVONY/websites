Word Unscrambler

Type your letters and see every word hiding in them, longest first. Use ? for a blank or unknown letter.

Advanced filters

Your words will appear here, longest first. Example: the letters EILNST make 90 words, including ELINTS, ENLIST and INLETS.

How it works

Letterpile checks your letters against the 172,823 words in the open ENABLE word list and keeps every word you could spell without reusing a tile. Results are grouped by length, so the long, high-value words are at the top.

Blanks and unknown letters

Put a ?, * or _ in your letters for each blank tile, up to three. Letters filled in by a blank are underlined with dots in the results. You can enter up to 15 letters plus the blanks.

Narrowing things down

  • Starts with / ends with helps when a word has to hook onto letters already on the board.
  • Contains finds words that pass through a letter or run of letters, like qu or ing.
  • Use every letter turns the unscrambler into a strict anagram finder.

Tap any word for its point value and an optional definition lookup.

What people use it for

Racks in tile games, the daily jumble, Text Twist rounds, crossword clues where you know the letters but not the order, and settling arguments about whether a word exists. Words come from the open ENABLE word list. Different games use different dictionaries, so always check the rules of your game.
