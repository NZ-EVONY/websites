9-Letter Words

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 24,848 entries are words with exactly 9 letters. That is about 14.4% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is QUIZZICAL (38 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 2,781 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • The most common endings here are -ES (3,282), -ED (2,315), -NG (2,301).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 11,728 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.0 letters.
  • More of them start with S than with any other letter (2,898).

Using these words

To find 9-letter words in your own letters, use the Word Unscrambler and set Length to 9 under Advanced filters. For example, the letters AAADKRRSV include AARDVARKS. Words come from the open ENABLE word list. Different games use different dictionaries, so always check the rules of your game.
