12-Letter Words Starting With V

Last updated: October 1, 2026

145 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 12-letter words that start with V (about 0.084%). Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is VANQUISHABLE (29 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 1 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
