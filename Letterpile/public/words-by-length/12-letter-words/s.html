Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,132 entries are 12-letter words that start with S. That is about 0.66% of the list. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is SUBJECTIVIZE (35 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 4 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate.
