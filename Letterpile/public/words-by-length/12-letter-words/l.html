12-Letter Words Starting With L

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 226 12-letter words that start with L in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.13% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is LEXICALIZING (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 2 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
