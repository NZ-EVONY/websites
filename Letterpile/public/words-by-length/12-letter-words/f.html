12-Letter Words Starting With F

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 333 12-letter words that start with F in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.19% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is FRIZZINESSES (33 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 3 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
