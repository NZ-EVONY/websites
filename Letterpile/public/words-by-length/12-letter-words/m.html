12-Letter Words Starting With M

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 654 12-letter words that start with M, about 0.38% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is MAXIMIZATION (32 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 4 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
