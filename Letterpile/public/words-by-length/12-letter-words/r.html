12-Letter Words Starting With R

Last updated: October 1, 2026

654 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 12-letter words that start with R (about 0.38%). All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZMATAZZES (50 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 1 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
