7-Letter Words

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 23,032 entries are words with exactly 7 letters. That is about 13.4% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is ZYZZYVA (43 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 7,632. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 7 read the same backward and forward, such as DEIFIED, HALALAH and REIFIER.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (2,112), -ED (2,070), -ER (1,816).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 10,709 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.0 letters.
  • More of them start with S than with any other letter (2,639).

Using these words

To find 7-letter words in your own letters, use the Word Unscrambler and set Length to 7 under Advanced filters. For example, the letters ABDEMNO include ABDOMEN. Words come from the open ENABLE word list. Different games use different dictionaries, so always check the rules of your game.
