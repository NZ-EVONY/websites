11-Letter Words

Last updated: October 1, 2026

15,487 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words with exactly 11 letters (about 9.0%). Every word here is the same length. The highest base score belongs to RAZZAMATAZZ (49 points), using commonly used tile values. 347 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • The most common endings here are -ES (2,518), -NG (1,759), -ED (1,171).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 7,199 of these words.
  • The average length is 11.0 letters.
  • More of them start with C than with any other letter (1,598).
