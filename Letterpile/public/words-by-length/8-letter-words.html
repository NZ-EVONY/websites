8-Letter Words

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 28,378 entries are words with exactly 8 letters. That is about 16.5% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, ZYZZYVAS (44 points) scores the most. 5,835 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • The most common endings here are -ES (2,764), -ED (2,547), -NG (2,045).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 15,288 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • More of them start with S than with any other letter (3,453).
