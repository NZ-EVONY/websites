9-Letter Words Starting With R

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,541 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 9-letter words that start with R (about 0.89%). All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is RHYTHMIZE (29 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 119 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
