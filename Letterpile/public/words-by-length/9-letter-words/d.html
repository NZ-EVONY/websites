9-Letter Words Starting With D

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,487 entries are 9-letter words that start with D. That is about 0.86% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to DRIZZLING (29 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 167. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
