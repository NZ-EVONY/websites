9-Letter Words Starting With U

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 726 entries are 9-letter words that start with U. That is about 0.42% of the list. Every word here is the same length. The highest base score belongs to UNMUZZLED (30 points), using commonly used tile values. 99 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
