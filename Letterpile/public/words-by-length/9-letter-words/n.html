9-Letter Words Starting With N

Last updated: October 1, 2026

534 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 9-letter words that start with N (about 0.31%). Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is NAZIFYING (25 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 38 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
