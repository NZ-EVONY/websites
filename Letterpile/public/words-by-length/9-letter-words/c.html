Last updated: October 1, 2026

2,351 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 9-letter words that start with C (about 1.4%). All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, CHAZZANIM (34 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 310. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
