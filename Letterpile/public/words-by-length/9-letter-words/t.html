9-Letter Words Starting With T

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,198 9-letter words that start with T, about 0.69% of the 172,434 words this site shows. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to TERRAZZOS (27 points), using commonly used tile values. 123 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
