9-Letter Words Starting With J

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 168 9-letter words that start with J in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.097% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, JAZZINESS (34 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 33. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
