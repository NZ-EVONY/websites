Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 905 entries are 10-letter words that start with T. That is about 0.52% of the list. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is TETRAZZINI (28 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 32.
