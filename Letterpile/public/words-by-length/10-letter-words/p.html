Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,801 10-letter words that start with P in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 1.0% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is PUZZLINGLY (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 119 have no repeated letter at all.
