10-Letter Words Starting With B

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 970 10-letter words that start with B in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.56% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is BLIZZARDLY (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 81 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
