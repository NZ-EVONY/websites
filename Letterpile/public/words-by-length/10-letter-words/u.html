10-Letter Words Starting With U

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 664 entries are 10-letter words that start with U. That is about 0.39% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to UNMUZZLING (31 points), using commonly used tile values. 36 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
