Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 791 10-letter words that start with O, about 0.46% of the 172,434 words this site shows. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, OUTDAZZLED (30 points) scores the most. 68 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
