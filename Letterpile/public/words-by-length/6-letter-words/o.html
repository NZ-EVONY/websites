6-Letter Words Starting With O

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 324 entries are 6-letter words that start with O. That is about 0.19% of the list. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is OFFKEY (19 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 164. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
