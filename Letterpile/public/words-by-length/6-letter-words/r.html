6-Letter Words Starting With R

Last updated: October 1, 2026

927 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 6-letter words that start with R (about 0.54%). Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZED (25 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 362 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
