6-Letter Words Starting With K

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 274 entries are 6-letter words that start with K. That is about 0.16% of the list. Every word here is the same length. Using commonly used tile values, KOLKOZ (23 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 102. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
