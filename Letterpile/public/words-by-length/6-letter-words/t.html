Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 900 6-letter words that start with T, about 0.52% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is TAZZAS (24 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 413 have no repeated letter at all.
