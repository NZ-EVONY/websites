6-Letter Words Starting With E

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 452 6-letter words that start with E in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.26% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is EXEQUY (25 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 151 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
