6-Letter Words Starting With I

Last updated: October 1, 2026

284 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 6-letter words that start with I (about 0.16%). All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, IZZARD (25 points) scores the most. 132 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
