6-Letter Words Starting With D

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 800 6-letter words that start with D in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.46% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to DAZZLE (25 points), using commonly used tile values. 368 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
