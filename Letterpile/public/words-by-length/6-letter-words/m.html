Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 788 6-letter words that start with M, about 0.46% of the 172,434 words this site shows. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is MIZZLY (29 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 406 have no repeated letter at all.
