Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 481 13-letter words that start with R in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.28% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZAMATAZZES (51 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 0.
