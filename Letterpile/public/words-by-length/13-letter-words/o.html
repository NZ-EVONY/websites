13-Letter Words Starting With O

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 305 entries are 13-letter words that start with O. That is about 0.18% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is OVEREMPHASIZE (32 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 0 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
