11-Letter Words Starting With N

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 437 11-letter words that start with N in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.25% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. Using commonly used tile values, NASOPHARYNX (26 points) scores the most. 6 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
