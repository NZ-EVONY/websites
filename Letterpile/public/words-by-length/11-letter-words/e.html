Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 710 11-letter words that start with E in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.41% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is EPOXIDIZING (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 13.
