11-Letter Words Starting With L

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 355 11-letter words that start with L in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.21% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is LIQUIDIZING (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 10 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
