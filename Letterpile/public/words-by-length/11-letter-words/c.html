Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,598 entries are 11-letter words that start with C. That is about 0.93% of the list. Every word here is the same length. Using commonly used tile values, CHIFFCHAFFS (33 points) scores the most. 42 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
