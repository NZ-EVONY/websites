Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 685 entries are 11-letter words that start with T. That is about 0.40% of the list. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is TRANQUILIZE (29 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 8 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
