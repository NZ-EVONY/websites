Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 808 7-letter words that start with L, about 0.47% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Every word here is the same length. The highest base score belongs to LOCKJAW (23 points), using commonly used tile values. 298 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
