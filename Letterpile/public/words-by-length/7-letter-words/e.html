7-Letter Words Starting With E

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 801 entries are 7-letter words that start with E. That is about 0.46% of the list. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is ENZYMIC (23 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 182 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
