7-Letter Words Starting With N

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 449 7-letter words that start with N, about 0.26% of the 172,434 words this site shows. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, NUZZLED (26 points) scores the most. 110 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
