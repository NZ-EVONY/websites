Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,352 entries are 7-letter words that start with T. That is about 0.78% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to TIZZIES (25 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 425.
