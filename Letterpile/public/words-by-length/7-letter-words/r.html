7-Letter Words Starting With R

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,384 7-letter words that start with R, about 0.80% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZING (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 342 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
