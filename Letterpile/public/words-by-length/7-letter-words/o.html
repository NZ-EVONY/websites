Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 617 7-letter words that start with O in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.36% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to OZONIZE (25 points), using commonly used tile values. 225 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
