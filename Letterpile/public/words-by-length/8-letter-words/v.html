Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 492 8-letter words that start with V in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.29% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, VIZCACHA (27 points) scores the most. 116 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate.
