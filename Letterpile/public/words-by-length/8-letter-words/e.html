8-Letter Words Starting With E

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,127 entries are 8-letter words that start with E. That is about 0.65% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to EMBEZZLE (30 points), using commonly used tile values. 144 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
