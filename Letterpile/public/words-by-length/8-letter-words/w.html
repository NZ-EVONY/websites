8-Letter Words Starting With W

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 728 entries are 8-letter words that start with W. That is about 0.42% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is WHIZZING (33 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 148 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
