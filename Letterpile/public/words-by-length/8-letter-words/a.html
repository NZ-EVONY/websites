8-Letter Words Starting With A

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,615 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 8-letter words that start with A (about 0.94%). Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is ALBIZZIA (28 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 252 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
