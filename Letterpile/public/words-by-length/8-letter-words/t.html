8-Letter Words Starting With T

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,525 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 8-letter words that start with T (about 0.88%). All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is TZITZITH (29 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 300 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
