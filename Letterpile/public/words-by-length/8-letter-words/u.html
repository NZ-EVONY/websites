8-Letter Words Starting With U

Last updated: October 1, 2026

825 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 8-letter words that start with U (about 0.48%). All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is UNMUZZLE (28 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 245 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
