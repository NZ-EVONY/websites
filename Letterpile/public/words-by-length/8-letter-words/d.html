Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,555 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 8-letter words that start with D (about 0.90%). Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is DIZZYING (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 310 have no repeated letter at all.
