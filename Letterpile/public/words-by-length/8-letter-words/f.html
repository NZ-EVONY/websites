Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,296 8-letter words that start with F in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.75% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is FRIZZILY (32 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 335 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
