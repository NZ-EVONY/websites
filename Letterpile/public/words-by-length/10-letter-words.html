10-Letter Words

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 20,284 words with exactly 10 letters in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 11.8% of the words Letterpile uses. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZMATAZZ (48 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 1,093 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • The most common endings here are -ES (3,388), -NG (2,277), -ED (1,802).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 9,857 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.0 letters.
  • More of them start with S than with any other letter (2,243).
