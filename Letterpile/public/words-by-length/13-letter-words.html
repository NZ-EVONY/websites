13-Letter Words

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 7,823 words with exactly 13 letters, about 4.5% of the 172,434 words this site shows. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZAMATAZZES (51 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 14 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,528), -NG (609), -LY (607).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 5, found in 3,416 of these words.
  • The average length is 13.0 letters.
  • More of them start with P than with any other letter (825).
