5-Letter Words Starting With I

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 118 5-letter words that start with I in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.068% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is IMMIX (16 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 70 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
