Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 554 entries are 5-letter words that start with P. That is about 0.32% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, PIZZA (25 points) scores the most. 389 have no repeated letter at all.
