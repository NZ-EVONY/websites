5-Letter Words Starting With A

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 495 5-letter words that start with A, about 0.29% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Every word here is the same length. The highest base score belongs to ABUZZ (25 points), using commonly used tile values. 278 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
