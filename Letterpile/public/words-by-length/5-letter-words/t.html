5-Letter Words Starting With T

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 543 entries are 5-letter words that start with T. That is about 0.31% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is TIZZY (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 357 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
