Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 661 5-letter words that start with C in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.38% of the words Letterpile uses. Every word here is the same length. By common tile values the top scorer is COZEY (19 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 470. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
