5-Letter Words Starting With W

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 295 entries are 5-letter words that start with W. That is about 0.17% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, WHIZZ (29 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 224. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
