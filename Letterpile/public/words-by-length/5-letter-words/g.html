5-Letter Words Starting With G

Last updated: October 1, 2026

412 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are 5-letter words that start with G (about 0.24%). All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is GAUZY (18 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 281. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
