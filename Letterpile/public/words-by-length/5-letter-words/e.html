5-Letter Words Starting With E

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 192 5-letter words that start with E, about 0.11% of the 172,434 words this site shows. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to ENZYM (19 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 90. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
