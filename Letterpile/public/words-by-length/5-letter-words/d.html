5-Letter Words Starting With D

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 449 entries are 5-letter words that start with D. That is about 0.26% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. By common tile values the top scorer is DIZZY (27 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 307. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
