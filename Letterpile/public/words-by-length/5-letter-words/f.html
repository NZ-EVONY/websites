Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 428 5-letter words that start with F, about 0.25% of the 172,434 words this site shows. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. The highest base score belongs to FIZZY (29 points), using commonly used tile values. 320 have no repeated letter at all.
