6-Letter Words

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 15,157 entries are words with exactly 6 letters. That is about 8.8% of the list. All of them have the same length, so they differ only in their letters. Using commonly used tile values, BEZAZZ (35 points) scores the most. 7,221 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • 7 read the same backward and forward, such as DENNED, HALLAH and MARRAM.
  • The most common endings here are -ED (1,495), -ER (1,264), -ES (1,075).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 9,127 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.0 letters.
  • More of them start with S than with any other letter (1,781).
