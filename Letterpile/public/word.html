Word pages

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Every word has more to it than its spelling. Type one above to see whether it's in our word list, what it scores with common tile values and with Words With Friends values, which other words use exactly the same letters, which shorter words hide inside it, which longer words contain it, and which single letters can be added to its front or back to make a new word. The details are worked out in your browser from the same open word list (ENABLE) that all of our tools use.

The 3,090 most common English words of three to eight letters also have a page of their own, listed below by first letter. Those pages add rhymes and syllable counts from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, and they're quick to share or bookmark: PLANET lives at /word/planet, for example. Any other word can be looked up here, and the address bar keeps it so you can share that too.

What each part means

  • Anagrams use every letter exactly once. LISTEN and SILENT are anagrams.
  • Words inside it use some of its letters, each no more often than it appears, in any order: the words you could make from those tiles.
  • Longer words containing it include it as an unbroken run of letters, the way PLANETARY contains PLANET.
  • Hooks are single letters added to one end to make another word. In board games they let you play a word alongside one that's already there.

Words the tools hide by default (vulgar words and slurs) don't get pages and aren't shown in these lists. Tap any word in a tool's results to see its points and hooks, and use the link in that window to come here.
