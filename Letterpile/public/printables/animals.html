Printable animal word searches

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Lions, giraffes, zebras and the rest of the savanna: an animal word search is the classic first puzzle for young readers, and a good way to practice spelling the names of animals they already know from books and zoo visits. These puzzles use the On Safari theme, from the big cats to the birds and grasses of the plains.

There are three puzzles below: a 10 × 10 grid for kids with 10 short words running across, down or diagonally; a 15 × 15 grid for teens with 16 words, some of them backward; and a 20 × 20 grid for adults with 27 words in all eight directions. Each PDF has the puzzle on page 1 and the answer key on page 2.

Pair one with a story about a safari or a zoo trip, then have children circle each animal as it comes up in the story.

Frequently asked questions

Are these word searches free to print?
Yes. Print as many copies as you like for home or classroom use.
Does every word appear only once?
Yes. Each puzzle is checked so that every listed word appears exactly once in the grid.
Are they safe for a classroom?
Yes. Rude words are never used, and the filler letters are checked so none can be read in any direction.
Can I get a different puzzle?
Use the printable puzzle maker: pick the On Safari theme and an age level, then press Make puzzle.
