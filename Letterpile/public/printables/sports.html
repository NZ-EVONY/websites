Printable sports word searches

Last updated: October 17, 2026

From soccer and swimming to relay races and gymnastics, the Sports Day theme brings together the games and gear of a school sports day and the Olympics alike.

There are three puzzles below: a 10 × 10 grid for kids with 10 short words running across, down or diagonally; a 15 × 15 grid for teens with 16 words, some of them backward; and a 20 × 20 grid for adults with 26 words in all eight directions. Each PDF has the puzzle on page 1 and the answer key on page 2.

Hand them out on a rainy day when sports are canceled, or before a sports day as a warm-up for the vocabulary.

Frequently asked questions

Are these word searches free to print?
Yes. Print as many copies as you like for home or classroom use.
Does every word appear only once?
Yes. Each puzzle is checked so that every listed word appears exactly once in the grid.
Are they safe for a classroom?
Yes. Rude words are never used, and the filler letters are checked so none can be read in any direction.
Can I get a different puzzle?
Use the printable puzzle maker: pick the Sports Day theme and an age level, then press Make puzzle.
