Printable food word searches

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Apples, bananas, mangoes and more: fruit names are short and familiar, so the Fruit Bowl theme suits even early readers at the kids level, while the adult puzzle digs into less common fruit.

There are three puzzles below: a 10 × 10 grid for kids with 8 short words running across, down or diagonally; a 15 × 15 grid for teens with 14 words, some of them backward; and a 20 × 20 grid for adults with 27 words in all eight directions. Each PDF has the puzzle on page 1 and the answer key on page 2.

Use it alongside a healthy-eating lesson, or set out real fruit and see who can find each one in the grid first.

Frequently asked questions

Are these word searches free to print?
Yes. Print as many copies as you like for home or classroom use.
Does every word appear only once?
Yes. Each puzzle is checked so that every listed word appears exactly once in the grid.
Are they safe for a classroom?
Yes. Rude words are never used, and the filler letters are checked so none can be read in any direction.
Can I get a different puzzle?
Use the printable puzzle maker: pick the Fruit Bowl theme and an age level, then press Make puzzle.
