Printable holiday word searches

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Holidays bring their own vocabulary: costumes and pumpkins in the fall, candles and wreaths in winter, fireworks and parades whenever there's something to celebrate. This set covers holidays from around the year and around the world, so the same puzzle works in October, December or spring.

There are three puzzles below: a 10 × 10 grid for kids with 10 short words running across, down or diagonally; a 15 × 15 grid for teens with 14 words, some of them backward; and a 20 × 20 grid for adults with 26 words in all eight directions. Each PDF has the puzzle on page 1 and the answer key on page 2.

Use one as a quiet activity at a class party, or tuck a printed copy into a holiday card for a child.

Frequently asked questions

Are these word searches free to print?
Yes. Print as many copies as you like for home or classroom use.
Does every word appear only once?
Yes. Each puzzle is checked so that every listed word appears exactly once in the grid.
Are they safe for a classroom?
Yes. Rude words are never used, and the filler letters are checked so none can be read in any direction.
Can I get a different puzzle?
Use the printable puzzle maker: pick the Holidays theme and an age level, then press Make puzzle.
