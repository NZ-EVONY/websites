Words Ending In B

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 242 entries are words that end in B. That is about 0.14% of the list. The longest is THINGAMABOB; the shortest is AB. Using commonly used tile values, COCKSCOMB (23 points) scores the most. 151 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 3 read the same backward and forward, such as BIB, BOB and BUB.
  • 49 leave another word when the final B is taken off, for example ALB, ARB, BARB, BIB and BIBB → AL, AR, BAR, BI and BIB.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 1, found in 123 of these words.
  • The average length is 5.2 letters.
  • Before B, the letter M appears most often (48 words).
