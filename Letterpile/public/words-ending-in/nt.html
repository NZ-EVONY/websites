Words Ending In NT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,967 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in NT (about 1.1%). They run from ANT at the short end to RADIOALLERGOSORBENT at the long end. The highest base score belongs to EMBEZZLEMENT (36 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 295. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 137 leave another word when the final NT is taken off, for example ABDUCENT, ACQUIESCENT, ADDUCENT, ADHERENT and AGENT → ABDUCE, ACQUIESCE, ADDUCE, ADHERE and AGE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 761 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.6 letters.
  • Before NT, the letter E appears most often (1,215 words).
