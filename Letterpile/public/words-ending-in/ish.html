Words Ending In ISH

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 554 words that end in ISH, about 0.32% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is CONTRADISTINGUISH; the shortest are DISH, FISH and PISH. The highest base score belongs to JACKFISH (27 points), using commonly used tile values. 229 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the suffix -ISH

The suffix ish has several related meanings. On adjectives and nouns it means “somewhat” or “like”: reddish, childish, bookish. It also forms words for nationalities and languages, which are capitalized in ordinary writing and so mostly absent from a lowercase word list like ENABLE. In casual speech it has become a word on its own, but that use is too new for most word lists.

Some common words end in I-S-H without carrying the suffix, such as fish, dish, wish and finish, and many verbs from French end this way too (polish, publish, vanish), where it is part of the verb rather than a suffix with its own meaning.

The statistics below count every word in the list that ends with these three letters, and the observations show how many leave a word when the ending is removed, a quick way to see how often -ish is a true suffix.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 271 leave another word when the final ISH is taken off, for example ACTORISH, AMATEURISH, ASPISH, BABYISH and BAIRNISH → ACTOR, AMATEUR, ASP, BABY and BAIRN.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 313 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.8 letters.
  • Before ISH, the letter F appears most often (122 words).
