Words Ending In H

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,932 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in H (about 1.1%). Lengths range from 2 letters (AH, EH and OH) to 21 (ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPH). By common tile values the top scorer is ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPH (36 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 804 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 5 read the same backward and forward, such as HAH, HALALAH and HALLAH.
  • 225 leave another word when the final H is taken off, for example AAH, AARRGHH, ABASH, AGOROTH and AIRTH → AA, AARRGH, ABAS, AGOROT and AIRT.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 832 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.4 letters.
  • Before H, the letter S appears most often (744 words).
