Words Ending In DS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

2,256 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in DS (about 1.3%). They run from ADS, IDS and ODS at the short end to ADRENOCORTICOSTEROIDS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, ISOCARBOXAZIDS (35 points) scores the most. 593 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 154 leave another word when the final DS is taken off, for example ACARIDS, ADDS, AIDS, ALANDS and ALKYDS → ACARI, AD, AI, ALAN and ALKY.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 874 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before DS, the letter I appears most often (457 words).
