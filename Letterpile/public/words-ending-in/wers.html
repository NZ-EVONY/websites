Words Ending In WERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 125 entries are words that end in WERS. That is about 0.072% of the list. Lengths range from 5 letters (EWERS) to 14 (BLANKETFLOWERS, PASSIONFLOWERS and THUNDERSHOWERS). By common tile values the top scorer is PASQUEFLOWERS (30 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 26. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 17 leave another word when the final WERS is taken off, for example AVOWERS, BOWERS, BURROWERS, DOWERS and HALLOWERS → AVO, BO, BURRO, DO and HALLO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 52 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.2 letters.
  • Before WERS, the letter O appears most often (94 words).
