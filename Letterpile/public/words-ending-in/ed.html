Words Ending In ED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 13,299 words that end in ED, about 7.7% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 2 letters (ED) to 20 (NONINSTITUTIONALIZED, OVERINTELLECTUALIZED and PARATHYROIDECTOMIZED). Using commonly used tile values, HYPOPHYSECTOMIZED (47 points) scores the most. 2,511 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

About the suffix -ED

The ending ed marks the past tense and past participle of regular verbs: walk, walked. It also forms adjectives from nouns, describing something that has a feature, as in bearded or talented. Irregular verbs such as go, see and take don't use it, which is why some common verbs are missing from this list.

The same spelling rules that shape -ing shape -ed: a final silent E merges with the ending (bake, baked), a final consonant after a short vowel is often doubled (stop, stopped), and a consonant plus Y becomes -ied (try, tried). Because nearly every regular verb has an -ed form, words ending in E-D make up a large slice of the word list, and the list is split here by the letter before E-D.

Some words end in these letters with no past-tense meaning at all, such as bed, red and seed; the counts include them.

Patterns in this list

  • 6 read the same backward and forward, such as DEED, DEIFIED and DEKED.
  • 5,975 leave another word when the final ED is taken off, for example AAHED, ABANDONED, ABASED, ABASHED and ABDUCTED → AAH, ABANDON, ABAS, ABASH and ABDUCT.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 4,734 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.8 letters.
  • Before ED, the letter T appears most often (2,875 words).
