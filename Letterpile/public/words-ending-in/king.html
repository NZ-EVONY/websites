Words Ending In KING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 643 entries are words that end in KING. That is about 0.37% of the list. Lengths range from 4 letters (KING) to 16 (COUNTERATTACKING). By common tile values the top scorer is BLACKJACKING (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 207 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
