Words Ending In EY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 252 entries are words that end in EY. That is about 0.15% of the list. The longest is INTERVALLEY; the shortest are BEY, DEY and FEY. Using commonly used tile values, HOKEYPOKEY (29 points) scores the most. 168 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 84 leave another word when the final EY is taken off, for example ALLEY, ATTORNEY, BAILEY, BOGEY and BURLEY → ALL, ATTORN, BAIL, BOG and BURL.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 175 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.1 letters.
  • Before EY, the letter K appears most often (44 words).
