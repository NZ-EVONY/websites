Words Ending In PERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

293 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in PERS (about 0.17%). The longest are SNIPPERSNAPPERS and WHIPPERSNAPPERS; the shortest is APERS. By common tile values the top scorer is WHIPPERSNAPPERS (29 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 41. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 133 leave another word when the final PERS is taken off, for example ASPERS, BACKSLAPPERS, BEBOPPERS, BEEPERS and BOPPERS → AS, BACKSLAP, BEBOP, BEE and BOP.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 153 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before PERS, the letter P appears most often (101 words).
