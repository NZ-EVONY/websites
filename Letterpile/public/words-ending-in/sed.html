Words Ending In SED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 831 entries are words that end in SED. That is about 0.48% of the list. Lengths range from 4 letters (USED) to 17 (COMPARTMENTALISED, IMMUNOCOMPROMISED and INSTITUTIONALISED). The highest base score belongs to IMMUNOCOMPROMISED (30 points), using commonly used tile values. 133 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 86 leave another word when the final SED is taken off, for example ABASED, ALKALISED, AMASSED, AMUSED and ASPERSED → ABA, ALKALI, AMAS, AMU and ASPER.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 313 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • Before SED, the letter I appears most often (328 words).
