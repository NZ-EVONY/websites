Words Ending In OING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

104 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in OING (about 0.060%). Lengths range from 5 letters (BOING, DOING and GOING) to 13 (FIANCHETTOING and THOROUGHGOING). The highest base score belongs to CHURCHGOING (23 points), using commonly used tile values. 14 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 29 leave another word when the final OING is taken off, for example BOOING, BUNKOING, CAMEOING, DADOING and DISCOING → BO, BUNK, CAME, DAD and DISC.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 48 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before OING, the letter G appears most often (20 words).
