Words Ending In CING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 255 entries are words that end in CING. That is about 0.15% of the list. Lengths range from 5 letters (ACING and ICING) to 18 (COUNTERINFLUENCING). The highest base score belongs to VIDEOCONFERENCING (29 points), using commonly used tile values. 45 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 28 leave another word when the final CING is taken off, for example ARCING, BRACING, DISCING, FACING and FARCING → AR, BRA, DIS, FA and FAR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 108 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.7 letters.
  • Before CING, the letter N appears most often (88 words).
