Words Ending In DING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 715 words that end in DING, about 0.41% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is COUNTERBLOCKADING; the shortest is DING. By common tile values the top scorer is HYPEREXTENDING (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 108 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 142 leave another word when the final DING is taken off, for example ABODING, ADDING, AIDING, AMENDING and BALDING → ABO, AD, AI, AMEN and BAL.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 306 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • Before DING, the letter N appears most often (208 words).
