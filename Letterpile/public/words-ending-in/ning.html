Words Ending In NING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

842 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in NING (about 0.49%). Lengths range from 6 letters (AWNING, BANING and BONING) to 19 (COUNTERCONDITIONING). Using commonly used tile values, COUNTERQUESTIONING (30 points) scores the most. 0 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
