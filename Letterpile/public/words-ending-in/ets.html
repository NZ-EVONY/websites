Words Ending In ETS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 626 words that end in ETS, about 0.36% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 4 letters (BETS, FETS and GETS) to 16 (ANTIFERROMAGNETS and FLIBBERTIGIBBETS). Using commonly used tile values, STRAIGHTJACKETS (32 points) scores the most. 176 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
