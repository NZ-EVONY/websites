Words Ending In ZER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 215 entries are words that end in ZER. That is about 0.12% of the list. They run from DOZER, GAZER and HAZER at the short end to INTELLECTUALIZER at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, QUIZZER (34 points) scores the most. 28 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 23 leave another word when the final ZER is taken off, for example BEEZER, BOOZER, BRAZER, COLONIZER and DOZER → BEE, BOO, BRA, COLONI and DO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 86 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • Before ZER, the letter I appears most often (164 words).
