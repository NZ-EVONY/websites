Words Ending In I

Last updated: October 1, 2026

793 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in I (about 0.46%). The longest are CINQUECENTISTI, PITHECANTHROPI and STREPTOBACILLI; the shortest are AI, BI and HI. By common tile values the top scorer is KOLKHOZNIKI (35 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 276. What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 147 leave another word when the final I is taken off, for example ABOMASI, AFGHANI, AMI, ANI and ANNULI → ABOMAS, AFGHAN, AM, AN and ANNUL.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 301 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.7 letters.
  • Before I, the letter L appears most often (116 words).
