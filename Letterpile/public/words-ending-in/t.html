Words Ending In T

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 8,455 words that end in T, about 4.9% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is OTORHINOLARYNGOLOGIST; the shortest are AT, ET and IT. Using commonly used tile values, PSYCHOPHARMACOLOGIST (40 points) scores the most. 2,172 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 8 read the same backward and forward, such as TAT, TENET and TERRET.
  • 743 leave another word when the final T is taken off, for example ABLEST, ABSOLUTEST, ACUTEST, ADJOINT and ADMIXT → ABLES, ABSOLUTES, ACUTES, ADJOIN and ADMIX.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 3,302 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.5 letters.
  • Before T, the letter S appears most often (3,348 words).
