Words Ending In RTS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 263 words that end in RTS, about 0.15% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 4 letters (ARTS and ORTS) to 14 (CONTAINERPORTS and COUNTEREFFORTS). The highest base score belongs to THOROUGHWORTS (23 points), using commonly used tile values. 83 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 31 leave another word when the final RTS is taken off, for example ABORTS, AIRTS, ALERTS, AVERTS and BOARTS → ABO, AI, ALE, AVE and BOA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 121 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.3 letters.
  • Before RTS, the letter O appears most often (115 words).
