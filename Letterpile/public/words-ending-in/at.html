Words Ending In AT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 337 entries are words that end in AT. That is about 0.20% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (AT) to 17 (LUMPENPROLETARIAT). By common tile values the top scorer is DIDDLYSQUAT (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 125. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as TAT.
  • 32 leave another word when the final AT is taken off, for example ASSIGNAT, BEAT, BEGAT, BOAT and CARAT → ASSIGN, BE, BEG, BO and CAR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 122 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.0 letters.
  • Before AT, the letter E appears most often (77 words).
