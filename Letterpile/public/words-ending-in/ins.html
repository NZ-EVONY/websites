Words Ending In INS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,004 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in INS (about 0.58%). Lengths range from 3 letters (INS) to 21 (ADRENOCORTICOTROPHINS). The highest base score belongs to DIPHENYLHYDANTOINS (34 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 281. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 143 leave another word when the final INS is taken off, for example ACTINS, ALANINS, ALBUMINS, ALUMINS and AMIDINS → ACT, ALAN, ALBUM, ALUM and AMID.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 413 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.8 letters.
  • Before INS, the letter A appears most often (128 words).
