Words Ending In LY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 6,374 entries are words that end in LY. That is about 3.7% of the list. The longest is ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHICALLY; the shortest are FLY, PLY and SLY. By common tile values the top scorer is ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHICALLY (47 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 949 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

About the suffix -LY

The ending ly most often turns an adjective into an adverb: quick becomes quickly, calm becomes calmly. It can also make adjectives from nouns, describing something “like” or “happening every”, as in friendly, costly and weekly. Many -ly words therefore come in families, with the base word sitting next to the longer form in the list.

Spelling changes are fewer than with -ing or -ed, but a few are regular: adjectives ending in a consonant plus Y change Y to I (happy, happily), and those ending in -le often become -ly by dropping the E (gentle, gently). Words such as fly, holy and ugly end in L-Y without being formed with the suffix.

Because the base word survives in most -ly words, the “leave another word” count below is high, which is a handy reminder for word puzzles: if you can see an adjective, an adverb is often two letters away.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 4,458 leave another word when the final LY is taken off, for example ABASEDLY, ABDOMINALLY, ABERRANTLY, ABHORRENTLY and ABIDINGLY → ABASED, ABDOMINAL, ABERRANT, ABHORRENT and ABIDING.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,537 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.6 letters.
  • Before LY, the letter L appears most often (1,979 words).
