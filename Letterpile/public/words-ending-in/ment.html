Words Ending In MENT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 616 entries are words that end in MENT. That is about 0.36% of the list. The longest are DISENFRANCHISEMENT and GEDANKENEXPERIMENT; the shortest is AMENT. Using commonly used tile values, EMBEZZLEMENT (36 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 33. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

About the suffix -MENT

The suffix ment turns verbs into nouns that name an action, its result or the means of doing it: develop, development; pay, payment; equip, equipment. Many of these nouns came into English from French, where the ending was already common.

The base verb usually keeps its spelling, including a silent E (move, movement), with a few well-known exceptions such as judgment in American spelling and argument, where the E of argue drops. That is why the share of words here that leave another word when the four letters are removed is fairly high, but not complete.

Words like cement, moment and comment end in M-E-N-T without a verb inside them that means the same thing. The page counts them all, so the numbers describe spelling patterns in the list rather than word history. Plural forms such as payments end in -ments and are counted on other ending pages.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 457 leave another word when the final MENT is taken off, for example ABANDONMENT, ABASEMENT, ABASHMENT, ABATEMENT and ABETMENT → ABANDON, ABASE, ABASH, ABATE and ABET.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 249 of these words.
  • The average length is 11.1 letters.
  • Before MENT, the letter E appears most often (223 words).
