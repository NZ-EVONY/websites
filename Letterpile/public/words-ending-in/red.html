Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,203 words that end in RED in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.70% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from RED at the short end to MALADMINISTERED, PREMANUFACTURED and SUPERCALENDERED at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is JACKHAMMERED (33 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 156 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
