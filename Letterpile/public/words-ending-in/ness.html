About the suffix -NESS

The suffix ness turns an adjective into a noun meaning “the state or quality of being”: kind, kindness; dark, darkness; aware, awareness. It is one of the most dependable suffixes in English: it is still used to make new words, it almost never changes the spelling of the base, and the meaning is easy to predict.

The one regular spelling change is the familiar Y to I after a consonant (happy, happiness). Because the base word nearly always survives intact, a very high share of the words on this page leave another word in the list when the four letters are removed, which you can check in the observations below.

Word lists also include plural forms such as kindnesses, which end in -nesses rather than -ness and so appear on other ending pages. For tile games, -ness is a long tail to hang on an adjective already on the board, where your game's dictionary allows it.
