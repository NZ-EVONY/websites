Words Ending In IERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 215 entries are words that end in IERS. That is about 0.12% of the list. They run from BIERS, KIERS and LIERS at the short end to PHOTOMULTIPLIERS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, HARQUEBUSIERS (27 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 27. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 49 leave another word when the final IERS is taken off, for example ANIMALIERS, BELIERS, BOMBARDIERS, BOULEVARDIERS and BRASIERS → ANIMAL, BEL, BOMBARD, BOULEVARD and BRAS.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 86 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.9 letters.
  • Before IERS, the letter F appears most often (60 words).
