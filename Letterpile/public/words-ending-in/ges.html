Words Ending In GES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 663 entries are words that end in GES. That is about 0.38% of the list. The longest is COUNTERESPIONAGES; the shortest is AGES. By common tile values the top scorer is HYPOPHARYNGES (30 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 140 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 77 leave another word when the final GES is taken off, for example ANECDOTAGES, ASPERGES, BADGES, BAREGES and BARGES → ANECDOTA, ASPER, BAD, BARE and BAR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 291 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • Before GES, the letter A appears most often (396 words).
