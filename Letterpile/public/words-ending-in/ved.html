Words Ending In VED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

215 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in VED (about 0.12%). They run from CAVED, COVED and DIVED at the short end to CRYOPRESERVED, INTERINVOLVED and PHOTOENGRAVED at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, INEQUIVALVED (28 points) scores the most. 63 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 30 leave another word when the final VED is taken off, for example BELIEVED, BRAVED, CARVED, CURVED and DELVED → BELIE, BRA, CAR, CUR and DEL.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 89 of these words.
