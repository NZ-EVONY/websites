Words Ending In NS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

6,115 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in NS (about 3.5%). They run from ENS, INS and ONS at the short end to OVERINTELLECTUALIZATIONS at the long end. The highest base score belongs to ANTHROPOMORPHIZATIONS (42 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 952. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 254 leave another word when the final NS is taken off, for example ABDUCENS, ACTINIANS, AGONS, AINS and AIRNS → ABDUCE, ACTINIA, AGO, AI and AIR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,236 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.6 letters.
  • Before NS, the letter O appears most often (3,668 words).
