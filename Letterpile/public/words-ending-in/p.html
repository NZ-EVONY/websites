Words Ending In P

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 949 words that end in P, about 0.55% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are HANDCRAFTSMANSHIP and INTERRELATIONSHIP; the shortest are OP and UP. By common tile values the top scorer is ACQUAINTANCESHIP (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 424 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
