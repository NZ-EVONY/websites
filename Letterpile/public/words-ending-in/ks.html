Words Ending In KS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,307 words that end in KS, about 0.76% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are COUNTERATTACKS and LAUGHINGSTOCKS; the shortest are ARKS, ASKS and AUKS. By common tile values the top scorer is KOLKHOZNIKS (35 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 413 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
