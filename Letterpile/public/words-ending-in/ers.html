Words Ending In ERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

5,799 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in ERS (about 3.4%). Lengths range from 3 letters (ERS) to 24 (ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHERS). The highest base score belongs to HEMIDEMISEMIQUAVERS (41 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 835. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 2,298 leave another word when the final ERS is taken off, for example ABANDONERS, ABASERS, ABOLISHERS, ABORTERS and ABSCONDERS → ABANDON, ABAS, ABOLISH, ABORT and ABSCOND.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 2,260 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • Before ERS, the letter T appears most often (1,135 words).
