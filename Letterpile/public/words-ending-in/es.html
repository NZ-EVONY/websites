Words Ending In ES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

22,874 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in ES (about 13.3%). They run from ES at the short end to ETHYLENEDIAMINETETRAACETATES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, RAZZAMATAZZES (51 points) scores the most. 2,402 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • 5 read the same backward and forward, such as SEES, SELLES and SEMEMES.
  • 6,064 leave another word when the final ES is taken off, for example ABACUSES, ABASES, ABASHES, ABATISES and ABATTISES → ABACUS, ABAS, ABASH, ABATIS and ABATTIS.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 6,928 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.4 letters.
  • Before ES, the letter S appears most often (5,357 words).
