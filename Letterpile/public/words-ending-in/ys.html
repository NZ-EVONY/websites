Words Ending In YS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 554 words that end in YS, about 0.32% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from AYS and BYS at the short end to RADIOIMMUNOASSAYS at the long end. The highest base score belongs to HOKEYPOKEYS (30 points), using commonly used tile values. 221 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
