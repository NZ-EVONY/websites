Words Ending In LING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,362 entries are words that end in LING. That is about 0.79% of the list. They run from LING at the short end to OVERCONTROLLING at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is ZIZZLING (36 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 227. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
