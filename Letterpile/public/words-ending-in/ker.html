Words Ending In KER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 396 words that end in KER, about 0.23% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from ASKER, BAKER and BIKER at the short end to COUNTERATTACKER at the long end. The highest base score belongs to KNICKERBOCKER (31 points), using commonly used tile values. 145 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 88 leave another word when the final KER is taken off, for example ASKER, BAKER, BALKER, BANKER and BARKER → AS, BA, BAL, BAN and BAR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 181 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.9 letters.
  • Before KER, the letter C appears most often (108 words).
