Words Ending In NED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 919 words that end in NED, about 0.53% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are COUNTERPETITIONED and COUNTERQUESTIONED; the shortest is SNED. Using commonly used tile values, COUNTERQUESTIONED (29 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 169. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as DENNED.
  • 163 leave another word when the final NED is taken off, for example AWAKENED, AWNED, BANED, BANNED and BINNED → AWAKE, AW, BA, BAN and BIN.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 325 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • Before NED, the letter I appears most often (232 words).
