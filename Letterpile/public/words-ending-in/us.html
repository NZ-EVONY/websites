Words Ending In US

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,794 words that end in US, about 1.0% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from US at the short end to PSEUDOPARENCHYMATOUS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, PSEUDOPARENCHYMATOUS (35 points) scores the most. 327 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as SULUS.

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 501 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.2 letters.
  • Before US, the letter O appears most often (1,201 words).
