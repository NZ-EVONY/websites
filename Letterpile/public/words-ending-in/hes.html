Words Ending In HES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 823 words that end in HES in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.48% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from HES at the short end to CONTRADISTINGUISHES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, KAFFEEKLATSCHES (34 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 125.
