Last updated: October 1, 2026

530 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in HING (about 0.31%). They run from OHING and THING at the short end to CONTRADISTINGUISHING at the long end. The highest base score belongs to CHROMOLITHOGRAPHING (36 points), using commonly used tile values. 134 have no repeated letter at all.
