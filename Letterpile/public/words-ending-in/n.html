Words Ending In N

Last updated: October 1, 2026

7,637 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in N (about 4.4%). The longest is OVERINTELLECTUALIZATION; the shortest are AN, EN and IN. Using commonly used tile values, ANTHROPOMORPHIZATION (41 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 1,601. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • 4 read the same backward and forward, such as NAAN, NAN and NOON.
  • 444 leave another word when the final N is taken off, for example ABELIAN, ACTINIAN, AGON, AIN and AIRN → ABELIA, ACTINIA, AGO, AI and AIR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,823 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.4 letters.
  • Before N, the letter O appears most often (3,802 words).
