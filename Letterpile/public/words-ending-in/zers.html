Words Ending In ZERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 214 words that end in ZERS, about 0.12% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from DOZERS, GAZERS and HAZERS at the short end to INTELLECTUALIZERS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, QUIZZERS (35 points) scores the most. 21 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 23 leave another word when the final ZERS is taken off, for example BEEZERS, BOOZERS, BRAZERS, COLONIZERS and DOZERS → BEE, BOO, BRA, COLONI and DO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 86 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.5 letters.
  • Before ZERS, the letter I appears most often (164 words).
