Words Ending In EST

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,807 entries are words that end in EST. That is about 1.0% of the list. They run from BEST, GEST and HEST at the short end to COUNTERPROTEST at the long end. The highest base score belongs to JAZZIEST (33 points), using commonly used tile values. 365 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the suffix -EST

The ending est forms the superlative of short adjectives, the “most” form: tall, tallest; kind, kindest. Longer adjectives usually use “most” instead (“most beautiful”), which is why superlatives in a word list cluster around short, common base words.

The spelling rules match the comparative -er: double the final consonant after a short vowel (hot, hottest), drop a silent E (wide, widest) and change a final Y after a consonant to I (busy, busiest). In older English, -est was also a verb ending used with “thou”, and some of those forms survive in word lists.

Not every word ending in E-S-T is a superlative: nest, chest, forest and interest are ordinary nouns. The counts on this page include them, and the “leave another word” figure shows how often removing the three letters gives back a base word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 392 leave another word when the final EST is taken off, for example ABRUPTEST, ABSTRACTEST, ABSURDEST, ACERBEST and ACRIDEST → ABRUPT, ABSTRACT, ABSURD, ACERB and ACRID.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,072 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.4 letters.
  • Before EST, the letter I appears most often (1,155 words).
