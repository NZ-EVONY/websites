Words Ending In MES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 325 entries are words that end in MES. That is about 0.19% of the list. They run from EMES at the short end to ULTRAMICROTOMES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to EXOENZYMES (31 points), using commonly used tile values. 73 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • 2 read the same backward and forward, such as SEMEMES and SEMES.
  • 42 leave another word when the final MES is taken off, for example AEROGRAMMES, ALMES, ANIMES, AXONEMES and BIOMES → AEROGRAM, AL, ANI, AXONE and BIO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 135 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.3 letters.
  • Before MES, the letter O appears most often (97 words).
