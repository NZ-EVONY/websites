Words Ending In HT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 223 entries are words that end in HT. That is about 0.13% of the list. They run from PHT at the short end to SUPERHEAVYWEIGHT and ULTRALIGHTWEIGHT at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, SUPERHEAVYWEIGHT (34 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 90. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 10 leave another word when the final HT is taken off, for example BAHT, BIGHT, BRIGHT, DIGHT and FIGHT → BA, BIG, BRIG, DIG and FIG.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 93 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.4 letters.
  • Before HT, the letter G appears most often (211 words).
