Words Ending In BER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

137 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in BER (about 0.079%). They run from AMBER, CABER and CUBER at the short end to BONNYCLABBER and NIGHTCLUBBER at the long end. The highest base score belongs to STOCKJOBBER (28 points), using commonly used tile values. 37 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 67 leave another word when the final BER is taken off, for example AMBER, BACKSTABBER, BARBER, BIBBER and BLABBER → AM, BACKSTAB, BAR, BIB and BLAB.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 86 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.2 letters.
  • Before BER, the letter B appears most often (55 words).
