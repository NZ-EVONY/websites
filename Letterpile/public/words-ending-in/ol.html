Words Ending In OL

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 232 entries are words that end in OL. That is about 0.13% of the list. The longest is TETRAHYDROCANNABINOL; the shortest are COL, DOL and MOL. The highest base score belongs to PENTYLENETETRAZOL (31 points), using commonly used tile values. 69 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 40 leave another word when the final OL is taken off, for example AMIDOL, AWOL, BANDEROL, BANNEROL and BORNEOL → AMID, AW, BANDER, BANNER and BORNE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 86 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.6 letters.
  • Before OL, the letter O appears most often (44 words).
