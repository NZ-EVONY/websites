Words Ending In UE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

231 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in UE (about 0.13%). They run from CUE, DUE and HUE at the short end to CHURRIGUERESQUE at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is MICROTECHNIQUE (32 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 82 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 38 leave another word when the final UE is taken off, for example AGUE, ANALOGUE, APOLOGUE, BATTUE and CATALOGUE → AG, ANALOG, APOLOG, BATT and CATALOG.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 80 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.3 letters.
  • Before UE, the letter Q appears most often (81 words).
