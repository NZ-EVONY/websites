Words Ending In LERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 530 words that end in LERS in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.31% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 6 letters (BALERS, FILERS and HALERS) to 14 (FORTUNETELLERS, KNUCKLEBALLERS and PSYCHOBABBLERS). Using commonly used tile values, EMBEZZLERS (32 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 82. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 165 leave another word when the final LERS is taken off, for example ANTLERS, ASHLERS, ASSAILERS, BAFFLERS and BALERS → ANT, ASH, ASSAI, BAFF and BA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 280 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before LERS, the letter L appears most often (118 words).
