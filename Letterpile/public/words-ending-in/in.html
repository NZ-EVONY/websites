Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 153 leave another word when the final IN is taken off, for example ACTIN, AGAIN, AGIN, ALANIN and ALBUMIN → ACT, AGA, AG, ALAN and ALBUM.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 430 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.8 letters.
  • Before IN, the letter A appears most often (146 words).
