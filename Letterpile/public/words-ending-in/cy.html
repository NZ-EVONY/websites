Words Ending In CY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

377 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in CY (about 0.22%). They run from ICY at the short end to COUNTERCONSPIRACY and COUNTERINSURGENCY at the long end. The highest base score belongs to GYROFREQUENCY (34 points), using commonly used tile values. 76 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 35 leave another word when the final CY is taken off, for example ABBACY, ABBOTCY, BANKRUPTCY, BARONETCY and BREVETCY → ABBA, ABBOT, BANKRUPT, BARONET and BREVET.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 171 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.4 letters.
  • Before CY, the letter N appears most often (254 words).
