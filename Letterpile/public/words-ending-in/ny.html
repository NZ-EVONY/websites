Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 63 leave another word when the final NY is taken off, for example AGONY, AWNY, BARNY, BENNY and BONY → AGO, AW, BAR, BEN and BO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 96 of these words.

  • Before NY, the letter O appears most often (94 words).
