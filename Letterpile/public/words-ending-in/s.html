Words Ending In S

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 65,314 entries are words that end in S. That is about 37.9% of the list. They run from AS, ES and IS at the short end to ETHYLENEDIAMINETETRAACETATES at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZAMATAZZES (51 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 10,078 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • 13 read the same backward and forward, such as SAGAS, SEES and SELLES.
  • 48,153 leave another word when the final S is taken off, for example AAHS, AALIIS, AALS, AARDVARKS and AAS → AAH, AALII, AAL, AARDVARK and AA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 18,536 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.7 letters.
  • Before S, the letter E appears most often (22,874 words).
