Words Ending In ING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 12,531 words that end in ING, about 7.3% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is OVERINTELLECTUALIZING; the shortest are DING, KING and LING. Using commonly used tile values, HYPOPHYSECTOMIZING (48 points) scores the most. 2,002 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the suffix -ING

The ending ing is the most familiar suffix in English. It forms the present participle of verbs (“she is reading”) and the gerund, the verb used as a noun (“reading is fun”). It also appears in plain nouns that have drifted away from their verbs, such as building and ceiling, and in many words where -ing is not a suffix at all, like king, sing and thing.

Spelling rules around -ing explain many of the patterns in this list: a silent E is usually dropped (make becomes making), a final consonant is often doubled after a short vowel (run becomes running), and verbs ending in -ie switch to -y (tie becomes tying). Because so many verbs take -ing, the list of words ending in I-N-G is very long and is split here by the letter that comes before it.

The share of the whole list that ends in -ing shows how strongly inflected forms shape any word list.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 6,530 leave another word when the final ING is taken off, for example AAHING, ABANDONING, ABASHING, ABASING and ABDUCTING → AAH, ABANDON, ABASH, ABAS and ABDUCT.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 4,584 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.7 letters.
  • Before ING, the letter T appears most often (2,603 words).
