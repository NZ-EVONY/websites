Words Ending In ET

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 662 entries are words that end in ET. That is about 0.38% of the list. The longest are ANTIFERROMAGNET and FLIBBERTIGIBBET; the shortest is ET. By common tile values the top scorer is STRAIGHTJACKET (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 243 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • 3 read the same backward and forward, such as TENET, TERRET and TET.
  • 183 leave another word when the final ET is taken off, for example ABET, ANCHORET, ANNULET, ARMET and AVOSET → AB, ANCHOR, ANNUL, ARM and AVOS.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 360 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.9 letters.
