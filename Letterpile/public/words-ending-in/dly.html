Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 392 entries are words that end in DLY. That is about 0.23% of the list. They run from IDLY at the short end to STRAIGHTFORWARDLY at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is BLIZZARDLY (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 90.   • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 127 of these words.
