Words Ending In UES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 209 words that end in UES in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.12% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 4 letters (CUES, DUES and HUES) to 15 (MICROTECHNIQUES). Using commonly used tile values, MICROTECHNIQUES (33 points) scores the most. 60 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 39 leave another word when the final UES is taken off, for example AGUES, ANALOGUES, APOLOGUES, BATTUES and CATALOGUES → AG, ANALOG, APOLOG, BATT and CATALOG.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 76 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.1 letters.
  • Before UES, the letter G appears most often (67 words).
