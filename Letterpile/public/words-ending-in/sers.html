Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 48 leave another word when the final SERS is taken off, for example ABASERS, AMASSERS, AMUSERS, ASPERSERS and BROWSERS → ABA, AMAS, AMU, ASPER and BROW.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 101 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before SERS, the letter I appears most often (46 words).
