Words Ending In EED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 161 entries are words that end in EED. That is about 0.093% of the list. Lengths range from 4 letters (DEED, FEED and GEED) to 12 (PICKERELWEED). Using commonly used tile values, CRAZYWEED (27 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 0. Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as DEED.
  • 30 leave another word when the final EED is taken off, for example ALLSEED, ANISEED, ANTEED, BIRDSEED and BUGSEED → ALLS, ANIS, ANT, BIRDS and BUGS.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 72 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.5 letters.
  • Before EED, the letter W appears most often (62 words).
