Words Ending In PES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 255 entries are words that end in PES. That is about 0.15% of the list. They run from PES at the short end to SPECTROHELIOSCOPES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to OPHTHALMOSCOPES (29 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 53. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 23 leave another word when the final PES is taken off, for example DOPES, ETAPES, FRAPPES, GRIPPES and HERPES → DO, ETA, FRAP, GRIP and HER.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 85 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • Before PES, the letter O appears most often (100 words).
