Words Ending In KES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 212 words that end in KES, about 0.12% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 4 letters (EKES, OKES and UKES) to 16 (MICROEARTHQUAKES). Using commonly used tile values, MICROEARTHQUAKES (36 points) scores the most. 71 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 33 leave another word when the final KES is taken off, for example AWAKES, BAKES, BETAKES, BIKES and BRAKES → AWA, BA, BETA, BI and BRA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 81 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • Before KES, the letter A appears most often (93 words).
