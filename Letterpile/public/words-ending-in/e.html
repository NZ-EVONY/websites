Words Ending In E

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 16,914 words that end in E, about 9.8% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is ETHYLENEDIAMINETETRAACETATE; the shortest are AE, BE and DE. Using commonly used tile values, CARBOXYMETHYLCELLULOSE (46 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 3,528. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 6 read the same backward and forward, such as EKE, EME and ERE.
  • 1,349 leave another word when the final E is taken off, for example ABASE, ABOLLAE, ABSCISSAE, ABSINTHE and ABYE → ABAS, ABOLLA, ABSCISSA, ABSINTH and ABY.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 6,045 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before E, the letter L appears most often (3,100 words).
