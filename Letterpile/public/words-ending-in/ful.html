Words Ending In FUL

Last updated: October 1, 2026

332 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in FUL (about 0.19%). The longest is DESSERTSPOONSFUL; the shortest are AWFUL and WOFUL. The highest base score belongs to FORETHOUGHTFUL (27 points), using commonly used tile values. 115 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

About the suffix -FUL

The suffix ful usually makes adjectives meaning “full of” or “having”: hopeful, careful, joyful. It also makes nouns of measurement, the amount that fills something: a spoonful, a handful, a cupful. Note the single L: the suffix is spelled with one L even though the word “full” has two.

Spelling changes are rare. A final Y after a consonant becomes I, as in beauty and beautiful, and otherwise the base usually stays as it is. That keeps the “leave another word” count on this page high.

The plural of a measurement noun is usually formed by adding S at the end (spoonfuls), and the older form with the S in the middle also exists in some word lists; check this list's entries before relying on either. Adverbs formed from -ful adjectives end in -fully and are counted on other pages, since they end in different letters.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 299 leave another word when the final FUL is taken off, for example AIDFUL, AIMFUL, ARMFUL, ARMSFUL and ARTFUL → AID, AIM, ARM, ARMS and ART.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 169 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.3 letters.
  • Before FUL, the letter E appears most often (68 words).
