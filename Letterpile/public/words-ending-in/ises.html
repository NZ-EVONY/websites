Words Ending In ISES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 567 words that end in ISES, about 0.33% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 5 letters (BISES, MISES and RISES) to 22 (ENCEPHALOMYOCARDITISES and KERATOCONJUNCTIVITISES). By common tile values the top scorer is KERATOCONJUNCTIVITISES (40 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 0 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 186 leave another word when the final ISES is taken off, for example ADVERTISES, AGONISES, AMORTISES, ANISES and APOLOGISES → ADVERT, AGON, AMORT, AN and APOLOG.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 229 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.1 letters.
  • Before ISES, the letter T appears most often (104 words).
