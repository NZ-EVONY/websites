Words Ending In ND

Last updated: October 1, 2026

531 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in ND (about 0.31%). They run from AND and END at the short end to CHATEAUBRIAND, COUNTERDEMAND and MISUNDERSTAND at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is HYPEREXTEND (27 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 192 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 35 leave another word when the final ND is taken off, for example ADHEREND, ALAND, BAND, BEND and BIND → ADHERE, ALA, BA, BE and BI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 212 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.7 letters.
  • Before ND, the letter A appears most often (208 words).
