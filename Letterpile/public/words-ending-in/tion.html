Words Ending In TION

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 2,583 words that end in TION in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 1.5% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 6 letters (ACTION, CATION and KATION) to 23 (OVERINTELLECTUALIZATION). By common tile values the top scorer is ANTHROPOMORPHIZATION (41 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 71 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the suffix -TION

The ending tion forms nouns of action or result, mostly from verbs of Latin origin: create, creation; inform, information; reduce, reduction. Strictly speaking the suffix is often -ion added to a stem that ends in T, but in spelling terms the four letters T-I-O-N are what you see, and that is what this page counts.

Because the verb and the noun often differ by more than just the ending (an E drops, a C becomes a T, or extra letters appear, as in explain and explanation), the “leave another word” count is lower here than for suffixes like -ness. Most -tion words are long and formal, and this is one of the endings where the longest entries in the list are found.

The pronunciation is the same “shun” sound as in -sion and -cian words, which end in different letters and are not included on this page.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 120 leave another word when the final TION is taken off, for example ALATION, AMMONIATION, ANIMATION, ARGUMENTATION and ASPHYXIATION → ALA, AMMONIA, ANIMA, ARGUMENTA and ASPHYXIA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 5, found in 821 of these words.
  • The average length is 12.2 letters.
  • Before TION, the letter A appears most often (1,988 words).
