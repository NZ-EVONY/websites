Words Ending In HS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 707 entries are words that end in HS. That is about 0.41% of the list. The longest is ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHS; the shortest is OHS. By common tile values the top scorer is ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHS (37 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 195 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as SHAHS.
  • 133 leave another word when the final HS is taken off, for example AAHS, AIRTHS, ALIYAHS, ALMAHS and ALMEHS → AA, AIRT, ALIYA, ALMA and ALME.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 269 of these words.

  • Before HS, the letter T appears most often (276 words).
