Words Ending In PING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 426 entries are words that end in PING. That is about 0.25% of the list. They run from PING at the short end to COUNTERSTEPPING and DAGUERREOTYPING at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is FELLOWSHIPPING (28 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 111. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 227 leave another word when the final PING is taken off, for example AIRDROPPING, BACKDROPPING, BACKSLAPPING, BACKSTOPPING and BARHOPPING → AIRDROP, BACKDROP, BACKSLAP, BACKSTOP and BARHOP.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 200 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.8 letters.
  • Before PING, the letter P appears most often (169 words).
