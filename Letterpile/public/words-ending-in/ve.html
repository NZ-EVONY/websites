Words Ending In VE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,200 entries are words that end in VE. That is about 0.70% of the list. The longest is POLYPHILOPROGENITIVE; the shortest are AVE and EVE. Using commonly used tile values, POLYPHILOPROGENITIVE (36 points) scores the most. 156 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
