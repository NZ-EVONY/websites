Words Ending In G

Last updated: October 1, 2026

13,044 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in G (about 7.6%). They run from AG at the short end to OVERINTELLECTUALIZING at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, HYPOPHYSECTOMIZING (48 points) scores the most. 2,282 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 2 read the same backward and forward, such as GAG and GIG.
  • 161 leave another word when the final G is taken off, for example ABSCISING, ACTING, AGING, AGOG and ALANG → ABSCISIN, ACTIN, AGIN, AGO and ALAN.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 4,723 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • Before G, the letter N appears most often (12,708 words).
