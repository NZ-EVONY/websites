Words Ending In NG

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 12,708 entries are words that end in NG. That is about 7.4% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (ENG) to 21 (OVERINTELLECTUALIZING). Using commonly used tile values, HYPOPHYSECTOMIZING (48 points) scores the most. 2,097 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 85 leave another word when the final NG is taken off, for example ALANG, ANTING, BANG, BANING and BINDING → ALA, ANTI, BA, BANI and BINDI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 4,630 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.6 letters.
  • Before NG, the letter I appears most often (12,531 words).
