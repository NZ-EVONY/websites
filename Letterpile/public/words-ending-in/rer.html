Words Ending In RER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 337 entries are words that end in RER. That is about 0.20% of the list. They run from AIRER, BARER and BORER at the short end to REMANUFACTURER at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, CONJECTURER (22 points) scores the most. 0 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 44 leave another word when the final RER is taken off, for example ABHORRER, ADORER, AIRER, BARER and BORER → ABHOR, ADO, AI, BA and BO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 226 of these words.
