Words Ending In BES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 101 entries are words that end in BES. That is about 0.059% of the list. The longest are CLAUSTROPHOBES, COMPUTERPHOBES and MISTRANSCRIBES; the shortest is OBES. Using commonly used tile values, COMPUTERPHOBES (27 points) scores the most. 32 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 19 leave another word when the final BES is taken off, for example ABBES, ADOBES, AEROBES, BABES and BARBES → AB, ADO, AERO, BA and BAR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 38 of these words.
