Words Ending In LED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,391 words that end in LED in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.81% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from LED at the short end to NONCREDENTIALED at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, ZIZZLED (35 points) scores the most. 308 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as DELED.
  • 421 leave another word when the final LED is taken off, for example ADDLED, AILED, AISLED, ANELED and ANNULLED → ADD, AI, AIS, ANE and ANNUL.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 532 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.2 letters.
  • Before LED, the letter L appears most often (348 words).
