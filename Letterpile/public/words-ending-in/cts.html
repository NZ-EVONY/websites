Words Ending In CTS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 240 words that end in CTS, about 0.14% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from ACTS at the short end to COUNTERPROJECTS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, COUNTERPROJECTS (28 points) scores the most. 48 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 16 leave another word when the final CTS is taken off, for example BISECTS, BRACTS, CONTRACTS, DECOCTS and DELICTS → BISE, BRA, CONTRA, DECO and DELI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 121 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • Before CTS, the letter E appears most often (110 words).
