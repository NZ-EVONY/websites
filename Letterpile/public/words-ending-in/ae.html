Words Ending In AE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 339 words that end in AE, about 0.20% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from AE at the short end to ICHTHYOFAUNAE, METACERCARIAE and MICROFILARIAE at the long end. The highest base score belongs to ZOOXANTHELLAE (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 71 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 48 leave another word when the final AE is taken off, for example ACALEPHAE, ALAE, ANTAE, ANTEFIXAE and AREAE → ACALEPH, AL, ANT, ANTEFIX and ARE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 155 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.5 letters.
  • Before AE, the letter L appears most often (89 words).
