Words Ending In FS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 273 words that end in FS in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.16% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 3 letters (EFS and IFS) to 13 (HANDKERCHIEFS, UNDERSHERIFFS and WEATHERPROOFS). The highest base score belongs to CHIFFCHAFFS (33 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 61. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 38 leave another word when the final FS is taken off, for example ALEFS, ARFS, BARFS, BEEFS and BELIEFS → ALE, AR, BAR, BEE and BELIE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 116 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.0 letters.
  • Before FS, the letter F appears most often (139 words).
