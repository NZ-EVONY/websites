Words Ending In VES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 512 words that end in VES in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.30% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 4 letters (AVES and EVES) to 18 (NONREPRESENTATIVES and ULTRACONSERVATIVES). By common tile values the top scorer is ARCHCONSERVATIVES (30 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 94 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 42 leave another word when the final VES is taken off, for example ABOVES, AGAVES, BEEVES, BELIEVES and BRAVES → ABO, AGA, BEE, BELIE and BRA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 166 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.2 letters.
  • Before VES, the letter I appears most often (266 words).
