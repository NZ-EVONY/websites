Words Ending In ON

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 3,802 entries are words that end in ON. That is about 2.2% of the list. The longest is OVERINTELLECTUALIZATION; the shortest is ON. By common tile values the top scorer is ANTHROPOMORPHIZATION (41 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 375 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
