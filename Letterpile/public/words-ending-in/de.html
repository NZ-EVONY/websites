Words Ending In DE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 702 entries are words that end in DE. That is about 0.41% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (DE) to 21 (PHOSPHOGLYCERALDEHYDE). By common tile values the top scorer is PHOSPHOGLYCERALDEHYDE (45 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 173. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 39 leave another word when the final DE is taken off, for example ABODE, AIDE, ALAMODE, AMIDE and BADE → ABO, AI, ALAMO, AMI and BA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 264 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.4 letters.
  • Before DE, the letter I appears most often (321 words).
