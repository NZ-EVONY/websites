Words Ending In PER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 301 entries are words that end in PER. That is about 0.17% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (PER) to 14 (SNIPPERSNAPPER and WHIPPERSNAPPER). The highest base score belongs to WHIPPERSNAPPER (28 points), using commonly used tile values. 63 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as REPAPER.
  • 134 leave another word when the final PER is taken off, for example ASPER, BACKSLAPPER, BEBOPPER, BEEPER and BOPPER → AS, BACKSLAP, BEBOP, BEE and BOP.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 156 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.5 letters.
  • Before PER, the letter P appears most often (103 words).
