Words Ending In GS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,630 words that end in GS in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.95% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is COUNTERCONDITIONINGS; the shortest are BAGS, BEGS and BIGS. Using commonly used tile values, WHIZZBANGS (37 points) scores the most. 398 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
