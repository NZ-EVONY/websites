Words Ending In TERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,135 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in TERS (about 0.66%). They run from AFTERS, ALTERS and ASTERS at the short end to MICROSPECTROPHOTOMETERS at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is MICROSPECTROPHOTOMETERS (38 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 123. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 274 leave another word when the final TERS is taken off, for example ABATERS, ABETTERS, ABUTTERS, ACQUITTERS and ADMITTERS → ABA, ABET, ABUT, ACQUIT and ADMIT.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 471 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • Before TERS, the letter S appears most often (254 words).
