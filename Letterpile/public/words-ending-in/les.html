Words Ending In LES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,415 words that end in LES, about 0.82% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from ALES and OLES at the short end to POLYACRYLONITRILES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, BENZIMIDAZOLES (37 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 326. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as SELLES.
  • 227 leave another word when the final LES is taken off, for example ABLES, ADDLES, AISLES, ANELES and ARLES → AB, ADD, AIS, ANE and AR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 502 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before LES, the letter B appears most often (235 words).
