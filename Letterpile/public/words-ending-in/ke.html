Words Ending In KE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 650 entries are words that end in KE. That is about 0.38% of the list. The longest are MICROEARTHQUAKE and UNSPORTSMANLIKE; the shortest are EKE, OKE and UKE. Using commonly used tile values, JAZZLIKE (37 points) scores the most. 220 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as EKE.
  • 36 leave another word when the final KE is taken off, for example AWAKE, BAKE, BETAKE, BIKE and BRAKE → AWA, BA, BETA, BI and BRA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 288 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • Before KE, the letter I appears most often (453 words).
