Words Ending In SES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 5,357 entries are words that end in SES. That is about 3.1% of the list. Lengths range from 4 letters (ESES, OSES and USES) to 23 (CARBOXYMETHYLCELLULOSES and INDISTINGUISHABLENESSES). The highest base score belongs to CARBOXYMETHYLCELLULOSES (47 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 0. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 207 leave another word when the final SES is taken off, for example ABASES, ABBESSES, ABYSSES, ADVENTURESSES and AGENESES → ABA, ABBES, ABYS, ADVENTURES and AGENE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 1,704 of these words.
  • The average length is 11.9 letters.
  • Before SES, the letter S appears most often (3,282 words).
