Words Ending In CED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 280 words that end in CED in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.16% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 4 letters (ACED and ICED) to 17 (COUNTERINFLUENCED). The highest base score belongs to PHOSPHORESCED (26 points), using commonly used tile values. 73 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 29 leave another word when the final CED is taken off, for example ARCED, BRACED, DISCED, FACED and FARCED → AR, BRA, DIS, FA and FAR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 121 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • Before CED, the letter N appears most often (92 words).
