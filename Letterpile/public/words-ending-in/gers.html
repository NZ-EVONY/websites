Last updated: October 1, 2026

275 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in GERS (about 0.16%). The longest is BRAUNSCHWEIGERS; the shortest are AGERS and EGERS. The highest base score belongs to BRAUNSCHWEIGERS (26 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 61.
