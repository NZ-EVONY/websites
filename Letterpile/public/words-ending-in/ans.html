Words Ending In ANS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

653 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in ANS (about 0.38%). They run from BANS, CANS and FANS at the short end to DISESTABLISHMENTARIANS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, RHYNCHOCEPHALIANS (35 points) scores the most. 132 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 86 leave another word when the final ANS is taken off, for example ACARIDANS, ALANS, ANNELIDANS, ANTIPODEANS and BANIANS → ACARID, AL, ANNELID, ANTIPODE and BANI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 175 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.2 letters.
  • Before ANS, the letter I appears most often (226 words).
