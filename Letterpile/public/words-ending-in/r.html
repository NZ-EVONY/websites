Words Ending In R

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 9,478 words that end in R in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 5.5% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from AR, ER and OR at the short end to ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHER at the long end. The highest base score belongs to HEMIDEMISEMIQUAVER (40 points), using commonly used tile values. 2,148 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • 8 read the same backward and forward, such as RADAR, REDDER and REFER.
  • 1,822 leave another word when the final R is taken off, for example ABASER, ABATER, ABIDER, ABJURER and ABLER → ABASE, ABATE, ABIDE, ABJURE and ABLE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 3,938 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • Before R, the letter E appears most often (7,465 words).
