Words Ending In NES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,330 words that end in NES, about 0.77% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 4 letters (ANES and ONES) to 25 (PHOSPHATIDYLETHANOLAMINES). The highest base score belongs to PHOSPHATIDYLETHANOLAMINES (44 points), using commonly used tile values. 250 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 83 leave another word when the final NES is taken off, for example ACARINES, ACHENES, AGENES, AGONES and ALKYNES → ACARI, ACHE, AGE, AGO and ALKY.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 501 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.6 letters.
  • Before NES, the letter I appears most often (692 words).
