Words Ending In ORS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 946 words that end in ORS, about 0.55% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 3 letters (ORS) to 20 (COUNTERDEMONSTRATORS). Using commonly used tile values, METHOXYCHLORS (33 points) scores the most. 122 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 244 leave another word when the final ORS is taken off, for example ABDUCTORS, ABSTRACTORS, ACCENTORS, ACCEPTORS and ACTORS → ABDUCT, ABSTRACT, ACCENT, ACCEPT and ACT.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 307 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.9 letters.
  • Before ORS, the letter T appears most often (646 words).
