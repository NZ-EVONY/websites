Words Ending In PS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 933 entries are words that end in PS. That is about 0.54% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (OPS and UPS) to 18 (HANDCRAFTSMANSHIPS and INTERRELATIONSHIPS). By common tile values the top scorer is ACQUAINTANCESHIPS (35 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 265 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 114 leave another word when the final PS is taken off, for example ALPS, AMPS, ASPS, BAPS and BEDLAMPS → AL, AM, AS, BA and BEDLAM.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 308 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.2 letters.
  • Before PS, the letter I appears most often (283 words).
