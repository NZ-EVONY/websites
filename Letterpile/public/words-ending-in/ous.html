Words Ending In OUS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,201 entries are words that end in OUS. That is about 0.70% of the list. They run from NOUS and SOUS at the short end to PSEUDOPARENCHYMATOUS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, PSEUDOPARENCHYMATOUS (35 points) scores the most. 128 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

About the suffix -OUS

The suffix ous forms adjectives meaning “full of” or “having the quality of”: danger, dangerous; fame, famous; joy, joyous. It came into English through French from Latin, and a large share of -ous words were borrowed whole rather than built in English, so many of them have no matching English base word.

That is why the “leave another word” figure on this page is much lower than for -ness or -less. Related endings such as -ious and -eous (various, gorgeous) still end in O-U-S and are counted here, which is one reason the list is longer than you might guess.

For word games, the vowel pair O and U makes -ous a handy way to use those two letters together at the end of a long word, and the letter before it is shown below, since it varies more than you might expect.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 213 leave another word when the final OUS is taken off, for example ADUNCOUS, ADVANTAGEOUS, ALBUMINOUS, ALUMINOUS and ANALOGOUS → ADUNC, ADVANTAGE, ALBUMIN, ALUMIN and ANALOG.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 5, found in 418 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.0 letters.
  • Before OUS, the letter I appears most often (270 words).
