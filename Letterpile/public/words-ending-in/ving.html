Words Ending In VING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 209 words that end in VING in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.12% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from CAVING, COVING and DIVING at the short end to CRYOPRESERVING, INTERINVOLVING and PHOTOENGRAVING at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is CRYOPRESERVING (25 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 54. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 30 leave another word when the final VING is taken off, for example BELIEVING, BRAVING, CARVING, CURVING and DELVING → BELIE, BRA, CAR, CUR and DEL.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 86 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.9 letters.
  • Before VING, the letter I appears most often (61 words).
