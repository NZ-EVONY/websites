Words Ending In LER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 595 words that end in LER, about 0.35% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from ABLER, BALER and FILER at the short end to FORTUNETELLER, KNUCKLEBALLER and PSYCHOBABBLER at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is EMBEZZLER (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 126 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 194 leave another word when the final LER is taken off, for example ABLER, AMPLER, ANTLER, ASHLER and ASSAILER → AB, AMP, ANT, ASH and ASSAI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 306 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.7 letters.
  • Before LER, the letter L appears most often (147 words).
