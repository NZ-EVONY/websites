Words Ending In AD

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 306 words that end in AD in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.18% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is TETRAMETHYLLEAD; the shortest is AD. By common tile values the top scorer is CHUCKLEHEAD (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 122 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as DAD.
  • 36 leave another word when the final AD is taken off, for example BALLAD, BEAD, BROAD, CHILIAD and COLEAD → BALL, BE, BRO, CHILI and COLE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 135 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.2 letters.
  • Before AD, the letter E appears most often (165 words).
