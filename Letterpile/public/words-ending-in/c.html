Words Ending In C

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 3,782 entries are words that end in C. That is about 2.2% of the list. The longest are ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHIC and MICROSPECTROPHOTOMETRIC; the shortest are ARC, DOC and HIC. By common tile values the top scorer is PSYCHOPHARMACOLOGIC (41 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 628.
