Words Ending In ST

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 3,348 entries are words that end in ST. That is about 1.9% of the list. Lengths range from 4 letters (BAST, BEST and BUST) to 21 (OTORHINOLARYNGOLOGIST). By common tile values the top scorer is PSYCHOPHARMACOLOGIST (40 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 629 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
