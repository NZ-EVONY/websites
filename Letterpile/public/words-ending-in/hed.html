Words Ending In HED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

576 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in HED (about 0.33%). They run from OHED and SHED at the short end to CONTRADISTINGUISHED at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, CHROMOLITHOGRAPHED (35 points) scores the most. 200 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 63 leave another word when the final HED is taken off, for example AAHED, ABASHED, AIRSHED, AIRTHED and ARCHED → AA, ABAS, AIRS, AIRT and ARC.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 212 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • Before HED, the letter C appears most often (232 words).
