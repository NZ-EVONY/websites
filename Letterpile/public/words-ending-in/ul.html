Words Ending In UL

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 376 words that end in UL, about 0.22% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from GUL and PUL at the short end to DESSERTSPOONSFUL at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, FORETHOUGHTFUL (27 points) scores the most. 142 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 12 leave another word when the final UL is taken off, for example AMPUL, BULBUL, CONSUL, HANGUL and HAUL → AMP, BULB, CONS, HANG and HA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 185 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • Before UL, the letter F appears most often (332 words).
