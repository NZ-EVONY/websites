Words Ending In LT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 142 entries are words that end in LT. That is about 0.082% of the list. The longest is COUNTERASSAULT; the shortest is ALT. By common tile values the top scorer is JACKSMELT (24 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 88 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 20 leave another word when the final LT is taken off, for example BELT, BOLT, DOLT, HALT and HILT → BE, BO, DO, HA and HI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 57 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.5 letters.
  • Before LT, the letter U appears most often (36 words).
