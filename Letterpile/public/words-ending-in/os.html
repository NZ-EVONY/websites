Last updated: October 1, 2026

891 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in OS (about 0.52%). Lengths range from 2 letters (OS) to 15 (PRONUNCIAMENTOS). Using commonly used tile values, INTERMEZZOS (31 points) scores the most. 257 have no repeated letter at all. Patterns in this list

  • 2 read the same backward and forward, such as SOLOS and SOS.
  • 193 leave another word when the final OS is taken off, for example ABOS, ADOS, ALTOS, ARMIGEROS and ASKOS → AB, AD, ALT, ARMIGER and ASK.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 330 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.4 letters.
  • Before OS, the letter T appears most often (120 words).
