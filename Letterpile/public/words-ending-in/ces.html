Words Ending In CES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,028 words that end in CES, about 0.60% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are COUNTERINTELLIGENCES, COUNTERSURVEILLANCES and COUNTERTRANSFERENCES; the shortest are ACES and ICES. Using commonly used tile values, CHEMILUMINESCENCES (31 points) scores the most. 122 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 404 of these words.
