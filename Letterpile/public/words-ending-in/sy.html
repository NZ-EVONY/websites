Words Ending In SY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 160 entries are words that end in SY. That is about 0.093% of the list. The longest is PHARMACOGNOSY; the shortest are BUSY, COSY and EASY. By common tile values the top scorer is NYMPHOLEPSY (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 71 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 60 leave another word when the final SY is taken off, for example ANTSY, ARTSY, BACKWOODSY, BALLSY and BASSY → ANT, ART, BACKWOOD, BALL and BAS.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 1, found in 76 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.5 letters.
  • Before SY, the letter S appears most often (34 words).
