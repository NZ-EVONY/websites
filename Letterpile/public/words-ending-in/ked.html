Words Ending In KED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 567 entries are words that end in KED. That is about 0.33% of the list. Lengths range from 4 letters (EKED) to 15 (COUNTERATTACKED). Using commonly used tile values, BLACKJACKED (33 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 266. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as DEKED.
  • 123 leave another word when the final KED is taken off, for example ANTICKED, ARCKED, ASKED, AWAKED and BAKED → ANTIC, ARC, AS, AWA and BA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 244 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.7 letters.
  • Before KED, the letter C appears most often (218 words).
