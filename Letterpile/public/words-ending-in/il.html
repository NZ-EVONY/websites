Words Ending In IL

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 292 words that end in IL, about 0.17% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from AIL, FIL and MIL at the short end to FLUOROURACIL and FORESTAYSAIL at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is CINQUEFOIL (24 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 160 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 49 leave another word when the final IL is taken off, for example ABOIL, ANIL, ARCHIL, ARIL and AVAIL → ABO, AN, ARCH, AR and AVA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 125 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.8 letters.
  • Before IL, the letter A appears most often (120 words).
