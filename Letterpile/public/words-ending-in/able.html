Words Ending In ABLE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,476 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in ABLE (about 0.86%). The longest is RADIOIMMUNOASSAYABLE; the shortest are CABLE, FABLE and GABLE. By common tile values the top scorer is HYDROLYZABLE (33 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 142 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

About the suffix -ABLE

The suffix able makes adjectives meaning “can be” or “worth being”: readable, washable, enjoyable. It is still productive, so new -able words appear regularly, and many of them are perfectly regular formations from verbs.

A few spelling rules apply. A silent E is often dropped (love, lovable), though it is kept after a soft C or G to keep the sound (change, changeable). Some words use the related ending -ible instead (visible, possible); those end in different letters and are not counted here. There is no simple rule for which ending a word takes, which is why both appear in spelling tests.

Several common words end in A-B-L-E without being formed with the suffix, such as table, cable and able itself. The figures below count every word in the list that ends with these four letters. Adverbs built on these adjectives end in -ably, as in comfortably, and appear on other pages.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 641 leave another word when the final ABLE is taken off, for example ABOLISHABLE, ABSORBABLE, ABSTRACTABLE, ACCEPTABLE and ACCOMPLISHABLE → ABOLISH, ABSORB, ABSTRACT, ACCEPT and ACCOMPLISH.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 607 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.2 letters.
  • Before ABLE, the letter T appears most often (284 words).
