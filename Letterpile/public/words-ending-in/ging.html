Last updated: October 1, 2026

438 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in GING (about 0.25%). They run from AGING at the short end to ULTRACENTRIFUGING at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is ZIGZAGGING (32 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 0 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
