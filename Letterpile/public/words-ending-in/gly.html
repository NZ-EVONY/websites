Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 517 words that end in GLY, about 0.30% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is INDISCRIMINATINGLY; the shortest are AGLY and UGLY. The highest base score belongs to DIZZYINGLY (36 points), using commonly used tile values. 74 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate.
