Words Ending In WN

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 159 entries are words that end in WN. That is about 0.092% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (AWN and OWN) to 11 (QUARTERSAWN and THISTLEDOWN). By common tile values the top scorer is KNOCKDOWN (23 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 80. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 22 leave another word when the final WN is taken off, for example ADOWN, BRAWN, BROWN, COMEDOWN and DOWN → ADO, BRA, BRO, COMEDO and DO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 78 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.1 letters.
  • Before WN, the letter O appears most often (113 words).
