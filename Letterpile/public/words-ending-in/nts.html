Words Ending In NTS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,400 entries are words that end in NTS. That is about 0.81% of the list. They run from ANTS at the short end to DISENFRANCHISEMENTS and GEDANKENEXPERIMENTS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, EMBEZZLEMENTS (37 points) scores the most. 169 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 77 leave another word when the final NTS is taken off, for example ADHERENTS, AGENTS, ALANTS, ANTECEDENTS and ASTRINGENTS → ADHERE, AGE, ALA, ANTECEDE and ASTRINGE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 535 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.6 letters.
  • Before NTS, the letter E appears most often (854 words).
