Words Ending In ER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 7,465 words that end in ER, about 4.3% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 2 letters (ER) to 23 (ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHER). Using commonly used tile values, HEMIDEMISEMIQUAVER (40 points) scores the most. 1,612 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

About the suffix -ER

The ending er has two main grammatical jobs. On verbs it names the person or thing that does the action: a baker bakes, a reader reads, an opener opens. On short adjectives it forms the comparative: tall, taller; quick, quicker. On top of that, a great many plain words simply end in these two letters, such as water, mother and never.

That mix makes words ending in E-R one of the largest two-letter endings in the list, so the page is split by the letter that comes before. Spelling rules again leave fingerprints: doubled consonants (big, bigger), dropped silent E (write, writer) and Y turning into I (happy, happier).

For players, an E and an R are two of the most useful tiles to keep together, because so many words can be extended with them.

Patterns in this list

  • 5 read the same backward and forward, such as REDDER, REFER and REIFIER.
  • 2,597 leave another word when the final ER is taken off, for example ABANDONER, ABASER, ABOLISHER, ABORTER and ABRUPTER → ABANDON, ABAS, ABOLISH, ABORT and ABRUPT.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 3,283 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • Before ER, the letter I appears most often (1,354 words).
