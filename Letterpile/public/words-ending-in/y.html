Words Ending In Y

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 13,270 words that end in Y, about 7.7% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from AY, BY and MY at the short end to ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHICALLY at the long end. The highest base score belongs to ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHICALLY (47 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 2,899. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as YAY.
  • 1,610 leave another word when the final Y is taken off, for example ABBEY, ABY, ACIDY, ACTRESSY and ADJUDICATORY → ABBE, AB, ACID, ACTRESS and ADJUDICATOR.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 3,249 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.7 letters.
  • Before Y, the letter L appears most often (6,374 words).
