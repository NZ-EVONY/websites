Words Ending In DERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 446 words that end in DERS in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.26% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 6 letters (ADDERS, AIDERS and ALDERS) to 14 (SUPERCALENDERS, SUPERCOLLIDERS and UNDERGROUNDERS). By common tile values the top scorer is OFFICEHOLDERS (25 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 79 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 105 leave another word when the final DERS is taken off, for example ADDERS, AIDERS, ALDERS, AMENDERS and ATTAINDERS → AD, AI, AL, AMEN and ATTAIN.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 199 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.0 letters.
  • Before DERS, the letter N appears most often (156 words).
