Words Ending In IER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,354 words that end in IER, about 0.79% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 4 letters (BIER, KIER and LIER) to 15 (PHOTOMULTIPLIER). Using commonly used tile values, JAZZIER (32 points) scores the most. 289 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as REIFIER.
  • 682 leave another word when the final IER is taken off, for example AIRIER, ANIMALIER, ANTSIER, ARTIER and ARTSIER → AIR, ANIMAL, ANTS, ART and ARTS.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 883 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.7 letters.
  • Before IER, the letter L appears most often (213 words).
