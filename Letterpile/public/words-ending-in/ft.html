Words Ending In FT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 117 words that end in FT in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.068% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 3 letters (AFT, EFT and OFT) to 12 (ANTIAIRCRAFT, GEMEINSCHAFT and GESELLSCHAFT). By common tile values the top scorer is GEMEINSCHAFT (23 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 59. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 15 leave another word when the final FT is taken off, for example ABAFT, DEFT, DELFT, HAFT and HEFT → ABA, DE, DEL, HA and HE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 38 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.2 letters.
  • Before FT, the letter A appears most often (55 words).
