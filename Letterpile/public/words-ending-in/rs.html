Words Ending In RS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 7,427 words that end in RS, about 4.3% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 3 letters (ARS, ERS and ORS) to 24 (ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHERS). The highest base score belongs to HEMIDEMISEMIQUAVERS (41 points), using commonly used tile values. 1,158 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 1,548 leave another word when the final RS is taken off, for example ABASERS, ABATERS, ABIDERS, ABJURERS and ABRADERS → ABASE, ABATE, ABIDE, ABJURE and ABRADE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 2,782 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.0 letters.
  • Before RS, the letter E appears most often (5,799 words).
