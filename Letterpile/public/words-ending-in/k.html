Words Ending In K

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,405 words that end in K in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.81% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from ARK, ASK and AUK at the short end to COUNTERATTACK, LAUGHINGSTOCK and THUNDERSTRUCK at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, KOLKHOZNIK (34 points) scores the most. 625 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
