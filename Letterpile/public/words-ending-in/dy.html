Words Ending In DY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

199 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in DY (about 0.12%). Lengths range from 4 letters (BODY, DIDY and EDDY) to 15 (ALLOTETRAPLOIDY and AUTOTETRAPLOIDY). The highest base score belongs to BLIZZARDY (33 points), using commonly used tile values. 87 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 70 leave another word when the final DY is taken off, for example BADDY, BALDY, BANDY, BENDY and BIDDY → BAD, BAL, BAN, BEN and BID.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 78 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.9 letters.
  • Before DY, the letter O appears most often (50 words).
