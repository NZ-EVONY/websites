Words Ending In GED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 455 words that end in GED, about 0.26% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 3 letters (GED) to 16 (ULTRACENTRIFUGED). Using commonly used tile values, ZIGZAGGED (31 points) scores the most. 98 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 175 leave another word when the final GED is taken off, for example BACKLOGGED, BADGED, BAGGED, BALLYRAGGED and BANGED → BACKLOG, BAD, BAG, BALLYRAG and BAN.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 179 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.1 letters.
  • Before GED, the letter G appears most often (122 words).
