Words Ending In AR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 707 entries are words that end in AR. That is about 0.41% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (AR) to 17 (POLYMORPHONUCLEAR and TEMPOROMANDIBULAR). Using commonly used tile values, POLYMORPHONUCLEAR (31 points) scores the most. 210 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 243 of these words.
