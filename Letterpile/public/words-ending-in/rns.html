Words Ending In RNS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 164 words that end in RNS in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.095% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from ERNS and URNS at the short end to FLUEGELHORNS, LAMELLICORNS and OVERCONCERNS at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is BLACKTHORNS (22 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 63. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 23 leave another word when the final RNS is taken off, for example ADORNS, AIRNS, ALTHORNS, BARNS and CASERNS → ADO, AI, ALTHO, BA and CASE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 87 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.8 letters.
  • Before RNS, the letter O appears most often (56 words).
