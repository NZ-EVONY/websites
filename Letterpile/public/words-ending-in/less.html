Words Ending In LESS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 647 entries are words that end in LESS. That is about 0.38% of the list. Lengths range from 5 letters (BLESS) to 16 (CONSTITUTIONLESS and INTERMISSIONLESS). Using commonly used tile values, QUENCHLESS (24 points) scores the most. 0 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the suffix -LESS

The suffix less means “without”: careless, hopeless, endless. It is the natural partner of -ful, and many base words take both, giving pairs like careful and careless. Like -ness, it is still freely used to coin new words, and it usually leaves the base word's spelling untouched.

That regularity shows up in the numbers: a large share of the words on this page remain a word when the last four letters are removed. The exceptions are mostly words where L-E-S-S is not a suffix at all, such as bless and unless.

Adverbs and nouns built on -less words, such as carelessly and carelessness, end in other letters and appear on other ending pages. In word games, -less can turn a short noun into a long adjective with four common letters, two of them S, so it is worth checking when you hold a pair of S tiles.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 629 leave another word when the final LESS is taken off, for example ACCENTLESS, ACTIONLESS, AFFECTIONLESS, AFFECTLESS and AGELESS → ACCENT, ACTION, AFFECTION, AFFECT and AGE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 306 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.8 letters.
  • Before LESS, the letter E appears most often (144 words).
