Words Ending In WS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 360 entries are words that end in WS. That is about 0.21% of the list. They run from BOWS, CAWS and COWS at the short end to PREINTERVIEWS and SCHOOLFELLOWS at the long end. The highest base score belongs to JACKSCREWS (28 points), using commonly used tile values. 116 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 41 leave another word when the final WS is taken off, for example AVOWS, BOWS, BRAWS, BROWS and BURROWS → AVO, BO, BRA, BRO and BURRO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 166 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.3 letters.
  • Before WS, the letter O appears most often (194 words).
