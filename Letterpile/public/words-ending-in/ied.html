Words Ending In IED

Last updated: October 1, 2026

502 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in IED (about 0.29%). Lengths range from 4 letters (DIED, GIED and HIED) to 15 (INTERSTRATIFIED). Using commonly used tile values, COMPLEXIFIED (29 points) scores the most. 61 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as DEIFIED.
  • 60 leave another word when the final IED is taken off, for example ALLIED, BANDIED, BELIED, BELLIED and BIRDIED → ALL, BAND, BEL, BELL and BIRD.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 192 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • Before IED, the letter F appears most often (217 words).
