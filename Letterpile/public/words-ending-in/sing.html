Words Ending In SING

Last updated: October 1, 2026

783 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in SING (about 0.45%). The longest are COMPARTMENTALISING, COUNTERADVERTISING and INSTITUTIONALISING; the shortest is SING. By common tile values the top scorer is SUBJECTIVISING (29 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 94 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 84 leave another word when the final SING is taken off, for example ABASING, ALKALISING, AMASSING, AMUSING and ASPERSING → ABA, ALKALI, AMAS, AMU and ASPER.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 287 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.1 letters.
  • Before SING, the letter I appears most often (327 words).
