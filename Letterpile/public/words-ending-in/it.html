Words Ending In IT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 342 words that end in IT in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.20% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is WHATCHAMACALLIT; the shortest is IT. Using commonly used tile values, WHATCHAMACALLIT (30 points) scores the most. 157 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 54 leave another word when the final IT is taken off, for example ADIT, ALIT, AWAIT, BAIT and BANDIT → AD, AL, AWA, BA and BAND.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 139 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.6 letters.
  • Before IT, the letter U appears most often (39 words).
