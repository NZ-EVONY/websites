Words Ending In CE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,005 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in CE (about 0.58%). Lengths range from 3 letters (ACE and ICE) to 19 (COUNTERINTELLIGENCE, COUNTERSURVEILLANCE and COUNTERTRANSFERENCE). Using commonly used tile values, CHEMILUMINESCENCE (30 points) scores the most. 154 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
