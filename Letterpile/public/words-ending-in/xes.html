Words Ending In XES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 286 words that end in XES in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.17% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 4 letters (AXES, EXES and OXES) to 16 (CHEMOPROPHYLAXES). The highest base score belongs to CHEMOPROPHYLAXES (40 points), using commonly used tile values. 60 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 29 leave another word when the final XES is taken off, for example APEXES, BORAXES, BOXES, DEXES and DUPLEXES → APE, BORA, BO, DE and DUPLE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 115 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.5 letters.
  • Before XES, the letter A appears most often (74 words).
