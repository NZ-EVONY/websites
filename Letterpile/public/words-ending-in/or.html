Words Ending In OR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 972 words that end in OR in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.56% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 2 letters (OR) to 19 (COUNTERDEMONSTRATOR). The highest base score belongs to METHOXYCHLOR (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 174 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 2 read the same backward and forward, such as ROTATOR and ROTOR.
  • 244 leave another word when the final OR is taken off, for example ABDUCTOR, ABSTRACTOR, ACCENTOR, ACCEPTOR and ACTOR → ABDUCT, ABSTRACT, ACCENT, ACCEPT and ACT.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 314 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.9 letters.
  • Before OR, the letter T appears most often (655 words).
