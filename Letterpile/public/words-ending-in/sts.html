Words Ending In STS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,543 entries are words that end in STS. That is about 0.89% of the list. The longest is OTORHINOLARYNGOLOGISTS; the shortest are BASTS, BESTS and BUSTS. By common tile values the top scorer is PSYCHOPHARMACOLOGISTS (41 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 0. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 64 leave another word when the final STS is taken off, for example ANIMISTS, BASSISTS, BASTS, BESTS and BOASTS → ANIMI, BASSI, BA, BE and BOA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 402 of these words.

  • Before STS, the letter I appears most often (1,182 words).
