Words Ending In DES

Last updated: October 1, 2026

753 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in DES (about 0.44%). They run from IDES and ODES at the short end to KERATOCONJUNCTIVITIDES and PHOSPHOGLYCERALDEHYDES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to PHOSPHOGLYCERALDEHYDES (46 points), using commonly used tile values. 113 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 39 leave another word when the final DES is taken off, for example ABODES, AEDES, AIDES, ALAMODES and AMIDES → ABO, AE, AI, ALAMO and AMI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 269 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.7 letters.
  • Before DES, the letter I appears most often (391 words).
