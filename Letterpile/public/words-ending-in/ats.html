Words Ending In ATS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

311 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that end in ATS (about 0.18%). Lengths range from 4 letters (BATS, CATS and EATS) to 18 (LUMPENPROLETARIATS). By common tile values the top scorer is ZIKKURATS (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 96 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as STATS.
  • 25 leave another word when the final ATS is taken off, for example ASSIGNATS, BEATS, BOATS, CARATS and CAVEATS → ASSIGN, BE, BO, CAR and CAVE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 109 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.1 letters.
  • Before ATS, the letter O appears most often (74 words).
