Words Ending In AS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 2,079 entries are words that end in AS. That is about 1.2% of the list. They run from AS at the short end to HYPERCHOLESTEROLEMIAS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, ZYZZYVAS (44 points) scores the most. 373 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as SAGAS.
  • 222 leave another word when the final AS is taken off, for example ABAS, AGAS, ALAS, ALBAS and ALUMINAS → AB, AG, AL, ALB and ALUMIN.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 741 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.2 letters.
  • Before AS, the letter I appears most often (528 words).
