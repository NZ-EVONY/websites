Words Ending In LTS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 105 words that end in LTS in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.061% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from ALTS at the short end to COUNTERASSAULTS at the long end. The highest base score belongs to JACKSMELTS (25 points), using commonly used tile values. 49 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 19 leave another word when the final LTS is taken off, for example BELTS, BOLTS, DOLTS, HALTS and HILTS → BE, BO, DO, HA and HI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 44 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.4 letters.
  • Before LTS, the letter U appears most often (30 words).
