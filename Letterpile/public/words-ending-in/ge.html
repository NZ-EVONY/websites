Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 673 words that end in GE in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.39% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 3 letters (AGE) to 16 (COUNTERESPIONAGE). By common tile values the top scorer is STOCKBROKERAGE (27 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 196. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 292 of these words.
