Words Ending In L

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 5,456 words that end in L, about 3.2% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 2 letters (AL and EL) to 22 (OTORHINOLARYNGOLOGICAL). Using commonly used tile values, PSYCHOPHARMACOLOGICAL (43 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 1,213. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as LEVEL.
  • 596 leave another word when the final L is taken off, for example AAL, ABDOMINAL, ABOMASAL, ACETAL and ACROMIAL → AA, ABDOMINA, ABOMASA, ACETA and ACROMIA.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,486 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • Before L, the letter A appears most often (3,534 words).
