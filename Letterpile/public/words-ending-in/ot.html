Words Ending In OT

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 315 entries are words that end in OT. That is about 0.18% of the list. The longest are SUPERPATRIOT and TROUBLESHOOT; the shortest are BOT, COT and DOT. Using commonly used tile values, MEZUZOT (27 points) scores the most. 109 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • 3 read the same backward and forward, such as TOOT, TOROT and TOT.
  • 35 leave another word when the final OT is taken off, for example ALLOT, BALLOT, BEGOT, BIGOT and BOOT → ALL, BALL, BEG, BIG and BO.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 125 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.7 letters.
  • Before OT, the letter O appears most often (80 words).
