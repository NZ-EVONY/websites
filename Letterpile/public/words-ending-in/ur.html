Words Ending In UR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 202 words that end in UR in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.12% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from BUR, CUR and FUR at the short end to CONGLOMERATEUR at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is ICHTHYOSAUR (22 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 86 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 28 leave another word when the final UR is taken off, for example ARBITRAGEUR, CHASSEUR, CLAQUEUR, COIFFEUR and CONGLOMERATEUR → ARBITRAGE, CHASSE, CLAQUE, COIFFE and CONGLOMERATE.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 69 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.2 letters.
  • Before UR, the letter O appears most often (80 words).
