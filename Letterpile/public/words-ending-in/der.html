Words Ending In DER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 517 entries are words that end in DER. That is about 0.30% of the list. Lengths range from 5 letters (ADDER, AIDER and ALDER) to 13 (SUPERCALENDER, SUPERCOLLIDER and UNDERGROUNDER). The highest base score belongs to OFFICEHOLDER (24 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 129. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as REDDER.
  • 125 leave another word when the final DER is taken off, for example ADDER, AIDER, ALDER, AMENDER and ATTAINDER → AD, AI, AL, AMEN and ATTAIN.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 236 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.9 letters.
  • Before DER, the letter N appears most often (170 words).
