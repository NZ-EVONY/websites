Words Ending In VERS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 210 words that end in VERS, about 0.12% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from AVERS and OVERS at the short end to HEMIDEMISEMIQUAVERS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, HEMIDEMISEMIQUAVERS (41 points) scores the most. 41 have no repeated letter at all. Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 33 leave another word when the final VERS is taken off, for example AIVERS, BELIEVERS, BRAVERS, CARVERS and DELIVERS → AI, BELIE, BRA, CAR and DELI.
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 97 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • Before VERS, the letter O appears most often (62 words).
