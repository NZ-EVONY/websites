About the Word of the Day

Each day at 00:00 UTC we pick one word that most people already know, and look at it a little more closely: what it means (sometimes more than one thing), how it's used in a sentence, and something about it that matters in word games, like an anagram hiding in its letters or a hook that turns it into a longer word. The words come from the 3,090 common words that have their own word pages, so every Word of the Day links to a page with its anagrams, the words inside it and its rhymes.

The notes on meaning and usage are written for Letterpile and checked before they're published. The word-game fact is worked out from our word list, the same open list (ENABLE) all our tools use, so it's always accurate for that list.

Why everyday words?

Plenty of sites offer a rare word a day. Everyday words are more useful in the games most people play: they're the answers daily puzzles choose, the words you can actually play on a board without a challenge, and they often hold more surprises than you'd expect. A familiar word with an anagram you'd never spotted, or one that takes a hook on both ends, is worth knowing.

Missed a day? The last week's words are listed above. For more, browse the curated word lists or try the training drills.
