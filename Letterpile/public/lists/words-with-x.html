Words with X

Last updated: October 17, 2026

X is the friendliest of the high-value letters. It's worth 8 with the common tile values (8 in Words With Friends), and it forms 5 two-letter words: AX, EX, OX, XI and XU. That makes it easy to place on a triple letter square so that it scores in two directions at once.

Our list has 4,584 words containing an X. This page shows all of them up to seven letters, then the longer X words you'd actually use in conversation.

Where X earns its points

  • Two ways at once. Put the X on a letter bonus where it forms AX one way and EX or OX the other, and the bonus counts in both words.
  • Prefixes. EX- starts hundreds of words, so an X next to an E is a long-word opportunity.
  • Endings. -X endings such as -AX, -EX, -IX and -OX close short words neatly.

Frequently asked questions

Which two-letter words use X?
AX, EX, OX, XI, XU, in the ENABLE list.
What does XU mean?
A former coin of Vietnam. Like many short words in game lists, it's rarely used outside them.
Why is X easier to play than Q or Z?
It forms several two-letter words with vowels, so it can almost always be placed alongside other tiles.
Are all X words listed?
Every one of seven letters or fewer, plus the longer ones on our everyday-word list. The full count is 4,584.
