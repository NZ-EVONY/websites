Words with J

Last updated: October 17, 2026

J is a heavy tile: 8 points with the common values and 10 in Words With Friends. It rarely appears more than once in a word, it almost always sits next to a vowel, and it has just 1 two-letter word in our list, JO. That makes the three-letter J words the workhorses: there are 25 of them, including JAB, JAW and JOT.

This page lists all 2,450 words with a J, shortest first, so the playable ones are always near the top.

Playing a J well

  • Find the vowel. J is followed by a vowel in almost every English word. Look for an open A, E, O or U on the board first.
  • Keep it for a bonus. A J on a triple letter square is worth 24 on its own.
  • Don't hold it too long. With so few short J words, waiting for the perfect spot can cost more than playing it now.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a two-letter word with J?
Yes, one: JO, a Scottish word for a sweetheart.
Why do so few words have two J's?
J came into English late, mostly through French and Latin, and it almost never doubles. Words with two J's are borrowed names and rare terms.
What's a good J word to remember?
The three-letter ones, like JAB, JAW, JOT, JOY and JUT, because they fit almost anywhere a vowel is open.
Does every game accept these words?
No list matches every game. These come from the open ENABLE list.
