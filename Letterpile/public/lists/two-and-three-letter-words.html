Two- and three-letter words

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Short words win games. They fill awkward gaps, let you lay a word alongside another so that several little words score at once, and get rid of a difficult letter for a few points instead of none. Strong players learn every two-letter word and most three-letter words by heart. Here are both lists in full: 96 two-letter words, from AA to YO, and 962 three-letter words, split into everyday words and the rest.

How players learn them

  • Two-letter words first. There are few enough to learn in a week. Group them by letter: the ones with X or J are worth memorizing first.
  • Then the strange three-letter words. You already know the everyday ones. The rare words in the second list are the ones that catch opponents out.
  • Practice with hooks. Many three-letter words are a two-letter word plus one letter; learning them together doubles the payoff.

Frequently asked questions

How many two-letter words are there?
96 in our list (ENABLE, without the hidden words). Other dictionaries have a few more or fewer.
Why are QI and ZA missing?
Some games accept them, but they aren't in the ENABLE list this site uses. Each game picks its own dictionary.
What makes a three-letter word everyday?
It's on a list of common English words (SCOWL, sizes 10 to 35).
Are one-letter words included?
No. Word games need at least two letters, and ENABLE starts at two.
