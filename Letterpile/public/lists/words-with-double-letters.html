Words with double letters

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Doubled letters trip up guessers and spellers alike. In five-letter puzzles, a double letter is a classic reason a sensible guess fails: the answer was APPLE or FLOOR and nobody thought to repeat a letter. Our list has 41,123 words with at least one doubled letter, far too many to read, so this page keeps the 1,808 very common ones and groups them by the letter that doubles, most frequent pairs first.

Patterns worth knowing

  • The usual doubles are L, E, S, O, T and F. SS, LL, EE lead this list.
  • Some letters almost never double in English: there's no common word with a doubled Q, X or J.
  • Triples are rare. Only 1 word in the whole list has the same letter three times in a row.
  • Two doubles. Words like COFFEE and COMMITTEE appear under each letter they double.

Frequently asked questions

Which letter doubles most often?
Among very common words, S: 329 words on this page have SS.
Why only very common words?
There are 41,123 words with a double letter in the whole list. Keeping the very common ones (SCOWL size 10 to 20) makes the page usable.
Do any words triple a letter?
A few: BRRR.
How do double letters affect daily puzzles?
A gray tile on a repeated letter only rules out extra copies. If you're stuck, try a guess that repeats a likely letter.
