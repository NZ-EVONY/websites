High-scoring short words

Last updated: October 17, 2026

A short word on the right square can outscore a long one in the middle of the board. These lists rank two-, three- and four-letter words by their points with the common tile values, highest first, with each word's score after it. Two-letter words scoring 5 or more, three-letter words scoring 8 or more and four-letter words scoring 12 or more are included. The top of each group: AX (9), ZAX (19), JAZZ (29).

How to use these

  • Aim for the bonus squares. Put the heavy letter on a double or triple letter square, and if the word also forms a second word, it scores twice.
  • Play parallel. Short words laid alongside an existing word form several two-letter words at once; every one of them scores.
  • Mind what you open. A short word next to a triple word square can hand your opponent a bigger play.

Frequently asked questions

What's the highest-scoring two-letter word?
AX, at 9 points with the common tile values, before bonus squares.
Do the scores include bonus squares?
No. They're the plain letter totals; bonus squares and crossing words add to them in a real game.
Why are some unfamiliar words here?
Short, high-scoring words are often borrowed or rare words. They're all in the ENABLE list; check your own game's dictionary.
Are Words With Friends scores different?
Yes. Several letters have different values there, so the order of these lists would change.
