Words with Q

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Q is worth 10 points with the usual tile values, and it's the letter players most often get stuck with. This page lists every word in our list that contains a Q: 2,525 of them, from QAT, QUA and SUQ up to the longest. The short ones matter most during a game, so they come first, and the rare words where Q stands without a U have their own section at the top.

When this list helps

  • Holding a Q without a U. The 29 words below are your only escape routes apart from swapping tiles.
  • Looking for a hook. QAT takes an S to make QATS, a cheap way to add a Q word to the board.
  • Five-letter puzzles. The 5-letter group is a quick way to check which Q words are possible when a puzzle shows a Q.

For the strategy behind playing a Q, read Words with Q but no U and High-value letters.

Frequently asked questions

What is the shortest word with Q?
In our list, the shortest have 3 letters: QAT, QUA, SUQ. QI, accepted by some games, isn't in the ENABLE list.
Which words use Q without U?
Our list has 29, shown at the top of this page; QAT, FAQIR and QWERTY are among the better known.
Does every game accept these words?
No. This is the open ENABLE list. Tournament and app dictionaries differ, so check unusual words against your game.
How many points is a Q worth?
10 with the common tile values and 10 with Words With Friends values.
