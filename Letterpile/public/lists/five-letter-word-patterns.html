Five-letter words by letter pair

Last updated: October 17, 2026

When a five-letter puzzle shows you two letters side by side, or you suspect a common pair like TH or CH, the possible answers shrink fast. These lists group the 8,564 five-letter words in our list by eleven of the most useful pairs. Within each pair, everyday words come first, because daily puzzles almost always choose a familiar answer; the rarer words follow.

Using these lists

  • Pairs, not positions. A word appears under a pair wherever the pair sits: THUMB, OTHER and TEETH are all under TH.
  • One word, several pairs. CHEEK is under both CH and EE.
  • Known positions? If you already know where letters go, the Wordle Solver or the Crossword Solver will narrow it further.

Frequently asked questions

Why are everyday words listed separately?
Daily puzzles pick familiar answers. Splitting the lists lets you try the likely words first and keep the rare ones for later.
What counts as an everyday word?
A word on a list of common English words (SCOWL, sizes 10 to 35). It's a guide, not a puzzle's answer list.
Can a word appear in more than one group?
Yes. Any word containing two of the pairs, like CHEEK, is listed under both.
Where are the other five-letter words?
The full list is on the 5-letter words page, linked below.
