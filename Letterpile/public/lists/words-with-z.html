Words with Z

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Z scores 10 with the usual tile values and 10 in Words With Friends, and unlike Q it needs no partner letter, so it fits into far more words. Our list has 7,019 words containing at least one Z. Most of the long ones are technical terms nobody plays, so this page lists every Z word of seven letters or fewer, then the longer Z words that are in everyday use.

Getting the most from a Z

  • Short is strong. Three-letter words like ZAX and ZOO let you put a Z on a letter or word bonus square without opening the board. (ZA, slang for pizza, is accepted by some games but isn't in our list.)
  • Double Z words such as JAZZ need two Z tiles or a blank; there are 66 of six letters or fewer, listed below.
  • Endings help: -IZE and -IZED turn many common verbs into Z words, which is why the 7-letter group is so long.

Frequently asked questions

What is the shortest Z word?
Our list has no two-letter Z word, so the shortest have three letters, like ZAX and ZOO. ZA is accepted by some games but isn't in ENABLE.
Why aren't all the long Z words listed?
There are 7,019 in total, mostly rare technical words. This page keeps the ones of seven letters or fewer and the everyday longer ones.
Which letters go well with Z?
Vowels most of all, and endings like -IZE and -IZED. ZO, ZA and ZE combinations start many short words.
Is ZEN a valid word?
Not in the ENABLE list, which leaves out most words usually written with a capital letter.
