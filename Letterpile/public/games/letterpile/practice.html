    How to play

    1. CATTrace a word through neighboring tiles, including diagonals. Drag, tap tile by tile, or type the letters. Words need 3 or more letters.
    2. DOGPlay it and its tiles vanish. Tiles above fall into the gaps; empty columns close up.
    3. ★Clear the pile in as few words as you can. Par is the target. Undo and restart are free, and up to 3 hints show where a word starts.

    About Practice mode

    Practice deals a fresh 5×5 pile whenever you press New board. Each pile comes from the same generator as the daily Letterpile: it's built backwards from common words, so it can always be cleared, and par is the number of words it was built from. Practice games aren't saved and don't affect your daily streak, so this is the place to try a new idea, like saving long words for last or clearing the bottom row first.

    Hints work the same way as in the daily game: each one lights up the first tile of a word that helps clear the pile. The Letterpile guide has strategy tips and worked examples.

    Things worth practicing

    Column collapse is the rule most players take a few games to use well. When a column empties, its neighbors slide together, so letters that were stranded far apart can suddenly touch. Try spotting a short word that runs straight up a column and asking what it would bring together. Watch the dimmed tiles too: a letter turns dim when no word in the list can use it with the tiles left, which usually means a Q, V or X lost its vowels a move or two earlier. Undo back to that point and look for a different order.
