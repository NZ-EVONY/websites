    How to play

    1. CATTrace a word through neighboring tiles, including diagonals. Drag, tap tile by tile, or type the letters. Words need 3 or more letters.
    2. DOGPlay it and its tiles vanish. Tiles above fall into the gaps; empty columns close up.
    3. ★Score big in 8 words. New tiles drop in from the top; long words score far more.

    How Arcade scoring works

    Arcade turns Letterpile into a race for points. You get a 6×6 pile and eight words. Every tile you clear is replaced by a new letter falling in from the top, chosen at random but weighted towards common letters, so the pile never runs out. Long words are worth far more than short ones:

    Word lengthPoints
    3 letters10
    4 letters20
    5 letters40
    6 letters70
    7 letters110
    8 letters160
    Each letter after 8+60

    Your best score is kept in this browser. Arcade words can't be undone, so look before you leap: a short word that drops good letters into place can set up a long one.

    The game ends after your eighth word, or sooner if no word can be traced anywhere on the board. Because fresh letters keep landing on top, a column never stays empty for long here; the real skill is reading which letters will tumble into the gaps a long word leaves behind.
