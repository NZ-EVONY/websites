      How to play

      Fill the white squares with letters so that every across and down entry answers its clue. Tap or click a square to select it; tap it again to switch between across and down. The highlighted squares show the current entry, and its clue appears in the bar under the grid and is highlighted in the clue lists. Tap any clue to jump to its entry.

      • Typing fills the square and moves to the next one. Backspace clears and steps back, the arrow keys move around the grid, Space switches direction, and Tab jumps to the next clue.
      • Check marks wrong letters in red, for the current letter, the current word or the whole puzzle.
      • Reveal fills in the right letter, word or the whole grid. Revealed squares are marked with a small triangle.

      On a phone, the on-screen keyboard opens when you select a square, and the clue bar sits just above it so you can always see the clue you're answering.

      About the puzzles

      Each puzzle uses the standard daily layout: a 15×15 grid whose black squares look the same when the grid is turned upside down, 70 to 78 entries, no entry shorter than three letters, and every letter part of both an across and a down word. Clues are written for Letterpile and checked before publishing; the same answer doesn't get the same clue twice within five weeks.

      Your letters, checks, reveals and time are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back later. The timer pauses while the page is hidden. When you finish, Share result copies your time without spoiling any answers.

      Frequently asked questions

      When does the crossword change?
      Every day at 00:00 UTC. The countdown above the grid shows how long is left.
      How do I switch between across and down?
      Tap the selected square again, press Space, or tap a clue in the other list.
      What do Check and Reveal do?
      Check marks wrong letters in red without fixing them. Reveal fills in the correct letters and marks them as revealed.
      Can I play an earlier crossword?
      Yes, the Daily Games page links to the last seven days' puzzles.
      Who writes the clues?
      The clues are written for this site and checked by hand before each puzzle is published. If you spot a mistake, please let us know.
