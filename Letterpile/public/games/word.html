How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Type a word with your keyboard or the letter buttons and press Enter. Each tile then changes to show how close you were:

  • Green with a check mark: the letter is in the word and in that spot.
  • Yellow with a dot: the letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
  • Gray: the letter isn't in the word, or not as many times as you used it.

The letter buttons take the same colors, so you can see at a glance which letters are left. Every guess must be a real word: the game accepts 8,564 five-letter words, but the answers are everyday words, never plurals ending in S and nothing rude.

Repeated letters

A word can contain the same letter twice. If you guess a word with two E's and the answer has only one, one E is colored and the other is gray. A gray tile on a repeated letter tells you there are no more copies than the ones already shown.

Streaks and sharing

Win on consecutive days to build a streak. Your statistics (games played, win percentage, current and best streak, and how many guesses your wins took) are kept in this browser only. When you finish, Share result copies a grid of colored squares that shows how you did without giving the word away.

Stuck partway? Today's hints give away one clue at a time, and the Wordle Solver lists every word that still fits your clues, and the guide to five-letter word strategy explains which first guesses test the most common letters.

Frequently asked questions

When does the word change?
Every day at 00:00 UTC, the same moment for everyone. The countdown above the board shows how long is left.
Can I play yesterday's word?
Yes. The Daily Games page links to the last seven days. Archive games don't change your streak.
Why was my word rejected?
Guesses must be five-letter words from the word list the game uses. Names, abbreviations and some rude words aren't accepted.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, in this browser's local storage, so a reload or a closed tab doesn't lose your guesses. Another device or browser starts fresh.
Is this the same puzzle as other daily word games?
No. Letterpile's answers are chosen from its own list of everyday words, so the answer is different from other sites' puzzles.
