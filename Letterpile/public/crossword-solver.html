Crossword Solver

Type the letters you know and a ? for each empty square. C?O?S finds CHOPS, CHOWS and CIONS and 15 more.

Matching words will appear here.

Writing a pattern

One character per square. Known letters as themselves; unknown squares as ?, _ or .. The pattern's length is the answer's length.

PatternFinds (in the ENABLE list)
?A?E128 four-letter words with A second and E last, such as BAKE, CAFE and GAME
????ING1,516 seven-letter words ending in ING
Q???? + exclude Ufive-letter Q words without a U: QAIDS, QANAT and QOPHS

Using the extra filters

Must include is for letters you know are in the answer but not where, from a crossing clue you half-remember. Exclude removes letters from the empty squares only, so a letter you've already placed is never excluded.

Answers with spaces, like phrases, aren't supported. Enter the letters without the gap. Words come from the open ENABLE word list. Different games use different dictionaries, so always check the rules of your game.
