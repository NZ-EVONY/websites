Words Starting With AF

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 231 entries are words that start with AF. That is about 0.13% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (AFF and AFT) to 16 (AFFECTLESSNESSES). Using commonly used tile values, AFFENPINSCHERS (27 points) scores the most. 13 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 77 still form a word when the leading AF is removed, for example AFAR, AFARS, AFEARED, AFFABLE and AFFAIR → AR, ARS, EARED, FABLE and FAIR.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (28), -ED (15), -TS (15).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 92 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.2 letters.
  • After AF, the letter F comes next most often (149 words).
