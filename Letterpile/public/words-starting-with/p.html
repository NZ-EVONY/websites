Words Starting With P

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 14,535 words that start with P in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 8.4% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from PA, PE and PI at the short end to PHOSPHATIDYLETHANOLAMINES at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is PHOSPHOGLYCERALDEHYDES (46 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 2,724 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 8 read the same backward and forward, such as PAP, PEEP and PEP.
  • 1,316 still form a word when the leading P is removed, for example PACE, PACED, PACES, PACING and PACT → ACE, ACED, ACES, ACING and ACT.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (2,058), -ED (930), -NG (907).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 4,625 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • After P, the letter R comes next most often (3,719 words).
