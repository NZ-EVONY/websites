Words Starting With F

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 6,740 entries are words that start with F. That is about 3.9% of the list. The longest is FORETHOUGHTFULNESSES; the shortest is FA. By common tile values the top scorer is FLEXOGRAPHICALLY (37 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 1,900 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 597 still form a word when the leading F is removed, for example FABLE, FABLER, FABLES, FACE and FACED → ABLE, ABLER, ABLES, ACE and ACED.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (888), -NG (503), -ED (486).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 2,224 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.5 letters.
  • After F, the letter O comes next most often (1,320 words).
