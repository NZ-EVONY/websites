Words Starting With UP

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 440 entries are words that start with UP. That is about 0.26% of the list. They run from UP at the short end to UPGRADEABILITIES, UPROARIOUSNESSES and UPSTANDINGNESSES at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is UPCHUCKING (24 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 187 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 384 still form a word when the leading UP is removed, for example UPAS, UPBEAR, UPBEARER, UPBEARERS and UPBEARING → AS, BEAR, BEARER, BEARERS and BEARING.
  • The most common endings here are -NG (82), -ED (53), -ES (39).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 221 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • After UP, the letter S comes next most often (88 words).
