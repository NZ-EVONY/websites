Words Starting With AP

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 678 words that start with AP, about 0.39% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are APPRECIATIVENESSES and APPREHENSIVENESSES; the shortest are APE and APT. Using commonly used tile values, APOSTROPHIZING (31 points) scores the most. 69 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 134 still form a word when the leading AP is removed, for example APACE, APACHE, APACHES, APART and APED → ACE, ACHE, ACHES, ART and ED.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (123), -NG (39), -ED (38).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 223 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • After AP, the letter P comes next most often (308 words).
