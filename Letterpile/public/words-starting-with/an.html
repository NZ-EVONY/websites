Words Starting With AN

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,960 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with AN (about 1.1%). They run from AN at the short end to ANTHROPOMORPHIZATIONS, ANTIAUTHORITARIANISMS and ANTIFERROMAGNETICALLY at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, ANTHROPOMORPHIZATIONS (42 points) scores the most. 189 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
