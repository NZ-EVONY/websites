Using commonly used tile values, TOXICOLOGICALLY (30 points) scores the most. 301 have no repeated letter at all. Patterns in this list

  • 3 read the same backward and forward, such as TOOT, TOROT and TOT.
  • 87 still form a word when the leading TO is removed, for example TOAD, TOADS, TOASTER, TOASTERS and TOBY → AD, ADS, ASTER, ASTERS and BY.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (161), -ED (69), -NG (69).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 417 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.9 letters.
  • After TO, the letter R comes next most often (164 words).
