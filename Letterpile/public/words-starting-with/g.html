Words Starting With G

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 5,574 words that start with G in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 3.2% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is GLOMERULONEPHRITIDES; the shortest is GO. The highest base score belongs to GEDANKENEXPERIMENTS (36 points), using commonly used tile values. 1,419 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • 2 read the same backward and forward, such as GAG and GIG.
  • 645 still form a word when the leading G is removed, for example GAB, GABLE, GABLES, GABOON and GABS → AB, ABLE, ABLES, ABOON and ABS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (743), -NG (363), -ED (360).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,753 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.3 letters.
  • After G, the letter R comes next most often (1,315 words).
