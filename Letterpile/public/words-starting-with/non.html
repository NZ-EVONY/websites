Words Starting With NON

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,566 words that start with NON in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.91% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is NONREPRESENTATIONALISMS; the shortest are NONA and NONE. By common tile values the top scorer is NONSCHIZOPHRENIC (37 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 0 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

About the prefix NON-

The prefix non means “not” and is the most neutral of the negative prefixes. Where un- can suggest something undone and dis- something reversed, non- usually just says a quality is absent: nonstop, nonfiction, nonsense. It is especially common in technical and formal vocabulary, and it is often written with a hyphen in everyday writing.

Word lists such as ENABLE contain only single unbroken words, so hyphenated forms do not appear, but many non- words that are commonly written solid are included. That gives this list an unusual shape: it holds a large share of long words, because non- tends to attach to words that are already long. The longest entries on this page show that clearly.

For puzzles, remember that N-O-N can also begin words that have no prefix, and that the double N appears when the base word itself starts with N, as in nonnative. Whether a given non- word is accepted in a game depends entirely on that game's dictionary.

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 1,511 still form a word when the leading NON is removed, for example NONABRASIVE, NONABSORBABLE, NONABSORBENT, NONABSORBENTS and NONABSORPTIVE → ABRASIVE, ABSORBABLE, ABSORBENT, ABSORBENTS and ABSORPTIVE.

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 452 of these words.
