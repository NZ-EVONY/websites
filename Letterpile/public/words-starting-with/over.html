Words Starting With OVER

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,675 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with OVER (about 0.97%). The longest is OVERINTELLECTUALIZATIONS; the shortest are OVERS and OVERT. The highest base score belongs to OVERCOMMERCIALIZATIONS (42 points), using commonly used tile values. 137 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

About the prefix OVER-

The prefix over usually means “too much” (overcook, overpay, overdue) or “above, across” (overhead, overpass). Like out-, it is one of English's most productive prefixes, and it combines freely with verbs, adjectives and nouns, so long over- words are plentiful.

Because the prefix is four letters long and the base words tend to be ordinary, over- words are a good illustration of how word length in a list is driven by prefixes and suffixes as much as by roots. Many of the longest words on this page are an over- prefix on an already long base. A word game player is unlikely to hold every letter needed for those, but shorter forms such as overt, overdo and overlap show up regularly.

Note that overt starts with the same four letters without being an over- word in meaning; the counts here are about spelling only.
