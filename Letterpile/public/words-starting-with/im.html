Words Starting With IM

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,122 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with IM (about 0.65%). They run from IMP at the short end to IMMUNOELECTROPHORETICALLY at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, IMMUNOCYTOCHEMICALLY (41 points) scores the most. 146 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
