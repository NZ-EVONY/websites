Last updated: October 1, 2026

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 41 still form a word when the leading LU is removed, for example LUBE, LUCENT, LUDE, LUES and LUETIC → BE, CENT, DE, ES and ETIC.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (73), -ED (27), -NG (27).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 158 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.9 letters.
  • After LU, the letter N comes next most often (83 words).
