Words Starting With T

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 8,730 entries are words that start with T. That is about 5.1% of the list. The longest is TETRAHYDROCANNABINOLS; the shortest are TA, TI and TO. Using commonly used tile values, THYROIDECTOMIZED (37 points) scores the most. 1,894 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • 8 read the same backward and forward, such as TAT, TENET and TERRET.
  • 765 still form a word when the leading T is removed, for example TAB, TABLE, TABLES, TABS and TACE → AB, ABLE, ABLES, ABS and ACE.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,210), -ED (561), -NG (550).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 2,817 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • After T, the letter R comes next most often (2,241 words).
