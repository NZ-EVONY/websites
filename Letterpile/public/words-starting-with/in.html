Words Starting With IN

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 4,510 entries are words that start with IN. That is about 2.6% of the list. They run from IN at the short end to INTERCOMPREHENSIBILITIES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to INTERCOMPREHENSIBILITY (36 points), using commonly used tile values. 385 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
