Words Starting With RU

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 514 entries are words that start with RU. That is about 0.30% of the list. The longest are RUDIMENTARINESSES and RUMBUSTIOUSNESSES; the shortest are RUB, RUE and RUG. The highest base score belongs to RUGGEDIZATIONS (26 points), using commonly used tile values. 163 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 49 still form a word when the leading RU is removed, for example RUANA, RUANAS, RUBABOO, RUBABOOS and RUBASSES → ANA, ANAS, BABOO, BABOOS and BASSES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (59), -NG (41), -ED (37).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 177 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.7 letters.
  • After RU, the letter B comes next most often (89 words).
