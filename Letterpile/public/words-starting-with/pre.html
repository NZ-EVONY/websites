Words Starting With PRE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,716 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with PRE (about 1.00%). Lengths range from 4 letters (PREE, PREP and PREX) to 19 (PREFIGURATIVENESSES and PRETERNATURALNESSES). The highest base score belongs to PREQUALIFICATIONS (33 points), using commonly used tile values. 108 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

About the prefix PRE-

The prefix pre comes from Latin and means “before”, either in time or in position. You see the time sense in preview, prepay and preheat, and the position sense in words such as prefix itself, which is a piece fixed before a word. Modern English keeps making new pre- words, so word lists include plenty of fairly technical ones alongside the everyday ones.

As with other prefixes, the spelling alone doesn't prove the meaning. Words such as press, pretty and prey start with P-R-E without any sense of “before”. The observations on this page count how many entries leave another word behind when the three letters are removed; that is a spelling test, not a history lesson, but it is a useful guide for players who want to know how often a P, R and E can be stuck onto the front of an existing word.

Players also meet pre- inside longer words, where the same three letters do no prefix work at all, so treat the counts as patterns of spelling rather than of meaning.
