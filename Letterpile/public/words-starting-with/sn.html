Words Starting With SN

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 513 words that start with SN, about 0.30% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is SNIPPERSNAPPERS; the shortest are SNAG, SNAP and SNAW. The highest base score belongs to SNAZZIEST (27 points), using commonly used tile values. 85 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
