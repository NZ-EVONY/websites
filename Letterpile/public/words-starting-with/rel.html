Words Starting With REL

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 277 words that start with REL in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.16% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from RELY at the short end to RELATIVISTICALLY and RELENTLESSNESSES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, RELINQUISHMENTS (29 points) scores the most. 41 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 34 still form a word when the leading REL is removed, for example RELACE, RELACED, RELACES, RELACING and RELAID → ACE, ACED, ACES, ACING and AID.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (42), -NG (39), -ED (37).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 95 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.0 letters.
  • After REL, the letter I comes next most often (92 words).
