Words Starting With COA

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 216 words that start with COA, about 0.13% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 4 letters (COAL, COAT and COAX) to 17 (COADMINISTRATIONS). By common tile values the top scorer is COADJUTRIX (27 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 52 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 19 still form a word when the leading COA is removed, for example COAGENT, COAGENTS, COALA, COALAS and COALED → GENT, GENTS, LA, LAS and LED.
  • The most common endings here are -ED (23), -NG (22), -ES (21).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 84 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.8 letters.
  • After COA, the letter L comes next most often (47 words).
