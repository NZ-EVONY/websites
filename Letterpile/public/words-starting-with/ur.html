Words Starting With UR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

224 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with UR (about 0.13%). Lengths range from 3 letters (URB, URD and URN) to 14 (URBANISTICALLY and UREDINIOSPORES). Using commonly used tile values, URBANIZATIONS (24 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 84. Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 29 still form a word when the leading UR is removed, for example URARE, URARES, URATE, URATES and URBAN → ARE, ARES, ATE, ATES and BAN.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (41), -IC (17), -AL (12).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 74 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • After UR, the letter E comes next most often (47 words).
