Words Starting With PY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 233 words that start with PY in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.14% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest are PYROELECTRICITIES and PYROMETALLURGICAL; the shortest are PYA, PYE and PYX. Using commonly used tile values, PYROLYZABLE (30 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 76. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 14 still form a word when the leading PY is removed, for example PYAS, PYEMIC, PYES, PYIN and PYINS → AS, EMIC, ES, IN and INS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (47), -IC (29), -AS (11).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 84 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.0 letters.
