Words Starting With IL

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 162 words that start with IL, about 0.094% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from ILK and ILL at the short end to ILLIMITABLENESSES, ILLUSIONISTICALLY and ILLUSTRIOUSNESSES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to ILLEGALIZATIONS (25 points), using commonly used tile values. 8 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 67 still form a word when the leading IL is removed, for example ILEX, ILEXES, ILKA, ILLAUDABLE and ILLAUDABLY → EX, EXES, KA, LAUDABLE and LAUDABLY.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (29), -LY (17), -AL (8).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 46 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.1 letters.
  • After IL, the letter L comes next most often (141 words).
