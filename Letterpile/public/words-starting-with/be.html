Words Starting With BE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,747 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with BE (about 1.0%). They run from BE at the short end to BENEFICIALNESSES, BENEVOLENTNESSES and BEWILDEREDNESSES at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is BENZODIAZEPINES (38 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 391 have no repeated letter at all.
