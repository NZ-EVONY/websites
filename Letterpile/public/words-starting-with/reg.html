Words Starting With REG

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 273 words that start with REG in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.16% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 3 letters (REG) to 16 (REGARDLESSNESSES, REGENERATENESSES and REGIONALIZATIONS). Using commonly used tile values, REGIONALIZATIONS (26 points) scores the most. 28 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 70 still form a word when the leading REG is removed, for example REGAIN, REGAINS, REGAL, REGALE and REGALES → AIN, AINS, AL, ALE and ALES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (34), -NG (34), -ED (31).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 112 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • After REG, the letter R comes next most often (73 words).
