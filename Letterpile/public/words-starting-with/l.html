Words Starting With L

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 5,048 words that start with L in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 2.9% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest are LYMPHOGRANULOMATOSES and LYMPHOGRANULOMATOSIS; the shortest are LA, LI and LO. The highest base score belongs to LEXICOGRAPHICALLY (37 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 1,439. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as LEVEL.
  • 295 still form a word when the leading L is removed, for example LAB, LABILITIES, LABILITY, LABS and LACE → AB, ABILITIES, ABILITY, ABS and ACE.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (683), -ED (318), -NG (310).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,593 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.2 letters.
  • After L, the letter A comes next most often (1,354 words).
