Words Starting With SUB

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,041 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with SUB (about 0.60%). They run from SUBA and SUBS at the short end to SUBCATEGORIZATIONS, SUBCLASSIFICATIONS and SUBCONSCIOUSNESSES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, SUBJECTIVIZATIONS (40 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 143. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the prefix SUB-

The prefix sub comes from Latin and means “under” or “below” (subway, submarine), and by extension “secondary” or “smaller part of” (subset, subplot). It is common in scientific and technical vocabulary, where it labels categories inside categories, so lists of sub- words lean toward the formal.

In older borrowings the B sometimes changed to match the next letter, which is why related Latin words begin with suc-, suf-, sug-, sup- or sus- instead; those words are not counted here, because this page is about the spelling S-U-B only. Plenty of other words start with these letters with no prefix at all, such as subtle in its modern sense and suburb in everyday use, so read the counts as a spelling pattern.

For players, sub- is a three-letter front hook worth knowing for a handful of everyday bases. Other everyday examples include subtotal and subway, where the meaning of the parts is still easy to see.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 727 still form a word when the leading SUB is removed, for example SUBABBOT, SUBABBOTS, SUBACID, SUBACIDLY and SUBACIDNESS → ABBOT, ABBOTS, ACID, ACIDLY and ACIDNESS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (157), -NG (67), -ED (66).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 351 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.3 letters.
  • After SUB, the letter S comes next most often (200 words).
