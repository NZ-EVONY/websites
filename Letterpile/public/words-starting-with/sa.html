Words Starting With SA

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,567 entries are words that start with SA. That is about 0.91% of the list. They run from SAB, SAC and SAD at the short end to SANCTIMONIOUSNESSES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to SAPROPHYTICALLY (30 points), using commonly used tile values. 275 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.
