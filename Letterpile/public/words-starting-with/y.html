Words Starting With Y

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 547 entries are words that start with Y. That is about 0.32% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (YA, YE and YO) to 14 (YOUTHFULNESSES). The highest base score belongs to YOUTHQUAKES (30 points), using commonly used tile values. 277 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as YAY.
  • 92 still form a word when the leading Y is removed, for example YAFF, YAGER, YAGERS, YAH and YAM → AFF, AGER, AGERS, AH and AM.
  • The most common endings here are -NG (46), -ED (41), -ER (36).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 237 of these words.
  • The average length is 6.5 letters.
  • After Y, the letter A comes next most often (176 words).
