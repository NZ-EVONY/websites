Words Starting With TR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 2,241 entries are words that start with TR. That is about 1.3% of the list. The longest is TRANSFORMATIONALISTS; the shortest is TRY. The highest base score belongs to TRISKAIDEKAPHOBIAS (34 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 401. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 120 still form a word when the leading TR is removed, for example TRACE, TRACED, TRACES, TRACING and TRACT → ACE, ACED, ACES, ACING and ACT.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (324), -ED (161), -NG (159).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 715 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.7 letters.
  • After TR, the letter A comes next most often (901 words).
