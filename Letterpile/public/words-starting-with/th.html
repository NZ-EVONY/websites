Words Starting With TH

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,171 words that start with TH in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.68% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest are THERMOELECTRICITIES, THERMOLUMINESCENCES and THERMOPERIODICITIES; the shortest are THE, THO and THY. The highest base score belongs to THYROIDECTOMIZED (37 points), using commonly used tile values. 280 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
