Words Starting With SQ

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 253 words that start with SQ in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.15% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from SQUAB, SQUAD and SQUAT at the short end to SQUEAMISHNESSES and SQUEEZABILITIES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to SQUEEZABILITY (36 points), using commonly used tile values. 41 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
