Words Starting With REP

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 622 words that start with REP, about 0.36% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are REPRESENTATIONALISMS, REPRESENTATIONALISTS and REPRESENTATIVENESSES; the shortest is REP. By common tile values the top scorer is REPUBLICANIZING (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 60. What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as REPAPER.
  • 122 still form a word when the leading REP is removed, for example REPAID, REPAIR, REPAIRED, REPAIRER and REPAIRERS → AID, AIR, AIRED, AIRER and AIRERS.
  • The most common endings here are -ED (87), -NG (86), -ES (80).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 210 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.0 letters.
  • After REP, the letter R comes next most often (169 words).
