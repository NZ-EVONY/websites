Words Starting With OP

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 334 words that start with OP, about 0.19% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 2 letters (OP) to 18 (OPHTHALMOLOGICALLY and OPINIONATIVENESSES). Using commonly used tile values, OPHTHALMOLOGICALLY (34 points) scores the most. 65 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 43 still form a word when the leading OP is removed, for example OPAH, OPAL, OPALINE, OPALINES and OPALS → AH, AL, ALINE, ALINES and ALS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (52), -LY (26), -ED (24).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 122 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.6 letters.
  • After OP, the letter E comes next most often (96 words).
