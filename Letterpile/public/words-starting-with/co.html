Words Starting With CO

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 6,419 words that start with CO, about 3.7% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are CONSTITUTIONALIZATIONS, COUNTERCOUNTERMEASURES and COUNTERINTERPRETATIONS; the shortest are COB, COD and COG. By common tile values the top scorer is COMPARTMENTALIZATIONS (38 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 964 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 739 still form a word when the leading CO is removed, for example COACERVATE, COACHED, COACHES, COACHING and COACT → ACERVATE, ACHED, ACHES, ACHING and ACT.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (880), -ED (485), -NG (482).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,892 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.2 letters.
  • After CO, the letter N comes next most often (1,946 words).
