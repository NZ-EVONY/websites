Words Starting With S

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 18,906 words that start with S in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 11.0% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 2 letters (SH, SI and SO) to 22 (SPECTROPHOTOMETRICALLY). The highest base score belongs to SCHIZOPHRENICALLY (41 points), using commonly used tile values. 3,283 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 13 read the same backward and forward, such as SAGAS, SEES and SELLES.
  • 2,043 still form a word when the leading S is removed, for example SAB, SABED, SABLE, SABLES and SABS → AB, ABED, ABLE, ABLES and ABS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (2,312), -NG (1,404), -ED (1,386).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 5,924 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.9 letters.
  • After S, the letter U comes next most often (2,882 words).
