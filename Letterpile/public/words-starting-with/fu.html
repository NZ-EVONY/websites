Words Starting With FU

Last updated: October 1, 2026

585 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with FU (about 0.34%). The highest base score belongs to FUZZINESSES (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 189 have no repeated letter at all.
