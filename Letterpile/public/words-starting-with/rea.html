Words Starting With REA

Last updated: October 1, 2026

476 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with REA (about 0.28%). The longest are REACCREDITATIONS, REAFFORESTATIONS and REAPPORTIONMENTS; the shortest are READ, REAL and REAM. The highest base score belongs to REACCLIMATIZING (31 points), using commonly used tile values. 46 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
