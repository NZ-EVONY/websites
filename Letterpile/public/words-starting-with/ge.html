Words Starting With GE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 688 entries are words that start with GE. That is about 0.40% of the list. The longest are GEDANKENEXPERIMENTS and GENTLEMANLIKENESSES; the shortest are GED, GEE and GEL. The highest base score belongs to GEDANKENEXPERIMENTS (36 points), using commonly used tile values. 110 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 61 still form a word when the leading GE is removed, for example GEANTICLINE, GEANTICLINES, GEAR, GEARS and GEED → ANTICLINE, ANTICLINES, AR, ARS and ED.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (97), -LY (35), -TS (35).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 193 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.3 letters.
  • After GE, the letter N comes next most often (213 words).
