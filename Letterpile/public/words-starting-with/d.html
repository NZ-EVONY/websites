Words Starting With D

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 10,122 words that start with D in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 5.9% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from DE and DO at the short end to DICHLORODIFLUOROMETHANES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to DIMETHYLHYDRAZINES (43 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 1,866. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • 9 read the same backward and forward, such as DAD, DEED and DEIFIED.
  • 487 still form a word when the leading D is removed, for example DAB, DABS, DACE, DACES and DAD → AB, ABS, ACE, ACES and AD.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,339), -ED (901), -NG (900).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 3,070 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.3 letters.
  • After D, the letter E comes next most often (3,753 words).
