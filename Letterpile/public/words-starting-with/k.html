Words Starting With K

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,712 words that start with K in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.99% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 2 letters (KA) to 22 (KERATOCONJUNCTIVITIDES and KERATOCONJUNCTIVITISES). Using commonly used tile values, KERATOCONJUNCTIVITIDES (41 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 507. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • 4 read the same backward and forward, such as KAIAK, KAYAK and KEEK.

  • The most common endings here are -ES (196), -ED (93), -NG (93).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 710 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.4 letters.
  • After K, the letter I comes next most often (469 words).
