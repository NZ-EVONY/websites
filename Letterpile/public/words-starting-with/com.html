Words Starting With COM

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 935 words that start with COM in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.54% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 4 letters (COMA, COMB and COME) to 21 (COMPARTMENTALIZATIONS). Using commonly used tile values, COMPARTMENTALIZATIONS (38 points) scores the most. 148 have no repeated letter at all. What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 233 still form a word when the leading COM is removed, for example COMAE, COMAL, COMAS, COMATE and COMATES → AE, AL, AS, ATE and ATES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (142), -NG (76), -ED (75).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 293 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.8 letters.
  • After COM, the letter P comes next most often (458 words).
