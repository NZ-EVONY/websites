Words Starting With TE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,375 words that start with TE in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.80% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is TETRAHYDROCANNABINOLS; the shortest are TEA, TED and TEE. Using commonly used tile values, TECHNICALIZATIONS (33 points) scores the most. 153 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
