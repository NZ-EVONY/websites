Words Starting With Q

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 822 entries are words that start with Q. That is about 0.48% of the list. They run from QAT and QUA at the short end to QUANTIFICATIONALLY, QUANTITATIVENESSES and QUASIPERIODICITIES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, QUIZZICALITY (44 points) scores the most. 240 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 3 still form a word when the leading Q is removed, for example QAID, QAIDS and QAT → AID, AIDS and AT.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (104), -NG (53), -ED (50).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 273 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.8 letters.
  • After Q, the letter U comes next most often (805 words).
