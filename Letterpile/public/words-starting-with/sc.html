Words Starting With SC

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,425 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with SC (about 0.83%). They run from SCAB, SCAD and SCAG at the short end to SCANDALMONGERINGS and SCHIZOPHRENICALLY at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is SCHIZOPHRENICALLY (41 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 268 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 487 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • After SC, the letter A comes next most often (336 words).
