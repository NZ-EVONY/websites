Words Starting With GY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 136 entries are words that start with GY. That is about 0.079% of the list. They run from GYM at the short end to GYNANDROMORPHISMS at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is GYROFREQUENCY (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 45 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 17 still form a word when the leading GY is removed, for example GYBE, GYBED, GYRALLY, GYRASE and GYRASES → BE, BED, RALLY, RASE and RASES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (22), -IC (8), -MS (7).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 1, found in 36 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.0 letters.
  • After GY, the letter R comes next most often (45 words).
