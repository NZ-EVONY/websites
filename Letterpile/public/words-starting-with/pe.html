Words Starting With PE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,921 words that start with PE in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 1.1% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 2 letters (PE) to 19 (PERSPICACIOUSNESSES). The highest base score belongs to PENTYLENETETRAZOLS (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 264 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
