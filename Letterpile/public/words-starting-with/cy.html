Words Starting With CY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 379 words that start with CY in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.22% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from CYAN, CYMA and CYME at the short end to CYTODIFFERENTIATIONS at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is CYCLOHEXYLAMINES (38 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 65 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

  • The most common endings here are -ES (71), -IC (32), -NS (26).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 100 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.9 letters.
