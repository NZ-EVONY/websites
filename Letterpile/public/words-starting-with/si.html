Words Starting With SI

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 945 words that start with SI, about 0.55% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 2 letters (SI) to 18 (SIMPLEMINDEDNESSES and SIMULTANEOUSNESSES). By common tile values the top scorer is SIZZLING (27 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 161 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
