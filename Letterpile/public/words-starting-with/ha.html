Words Starting With HA

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,538 words that start with HA, about 0.89% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from HA at the short end to HANDCRAFTSMANSHIPS at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is HANDCRAFTSMANSHIPS (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 483 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
