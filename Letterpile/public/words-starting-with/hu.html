Words Starting With HU

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 433 entries are words that start with HU. That is about 0.25% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (HUB, HUE and HUG) to 16 (HUMANITARIANISMS). By common tile values the top scorer is HUZZAHING (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 154. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as HUH.
  • 30 still form a word when the leading HU is removed, for example HUBRIS, HUED, HUES, HUGEST and HULA → BRIS, ED, ES, GEST and LA.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (52), -ED (43), -NG (39).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 168 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.7 letters.
  • After HU, the letter M comes next most often (156 words).
