Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 2,368 words that start with PA, about 1.4% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The highest base score belongs to PARATHYROIDECTOMIZED (43 points), using commonly used tile values. 494 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
