Words Starting With RE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 6,674 words that start with RE, about 3.9% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 2 letters (RE) to 23 (REINSTITUTIONALIZATIONS). The highest base score belongs to RECONTEXTUALIZING (36 points), using commonly used tile values. 602 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

About the prefix RE-

The prefix re usually means “again” (reread, rebuild, reheat) or “back” (repay, return). It attaches to a huge number of verbs, which is why the list of words starting with R-E is one of the longest two-letter starts in the whole word list and has to be split into smaller pages here.

Many words that start with these letters have no living prefix, though. In record, regal or reason the opening letters are just part of the word. A quick test is to cover the first two letters: if what remains is a word with a related meaning, the prefix is probably real. The “still form a word” figure below applies a strict spelling version of that test, so it also catches accidental cases such as a word that happens to contain a shorter, unrelated word.

In tile games, a spare R and E are among the most flexible letters to hold, and re- extensions are a common way to lengthen a word already on the board, though only where your game's dictionary accepts the longer form.

Useful observations

  • 5 read the same backward and forward, such as REDDER, REFER and REIFIER.
  • 4,230 still form a word when the leading RE is removed, for example REABSORB, REABSORBED, REABSORBING, REABSORBS and REACCEDE → ABSORB, ABSORBED, ABSORBING, ABSORBS and ACCEDE.
  • The most common endings here are -NG (1,000), -ED (951), -ES (815).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 2,221 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.6 letters.
  • After RE, the letter S comes next most often (832 words).
