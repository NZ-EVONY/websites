Words Starting With DIS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,690 words that start with DIS in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.98% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from DISC, DISH and DISK at the short end to DISESTABLISHMENTARIANS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, DISQUALIFICATIONS (32 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 79. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the prefix DIS-

The prefix dis generally marks an opposite, an absence or a removal. Compare agree and disagree, honest and dishonest, arm and disarm. Most dis- words came into English through Latin or French, and many of them were formed in those languages rather than in English, which is why some of them have no matching English base word: distract and dismal, for instance, don't break neatly into dis- plus a modern word.

On this page every word starting with D-I-S is counted, prefix or not, and the observations show how many still spell a word once those three letters are taken away. Expect a mix of clean pairs and coincidences. For word puzzles, dis- is worth remembering as a way to reach seven or eight letters from a four- or five-letter base, and the S in the middle means that a single S on your rack can sometimes be used inside the prefix rather than as a plural.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 1,079 still form a word when the leading DIS is removed, for example DISABILITIES, DISABILITY, DISABLE, DISABLES and DISABUSE → ABILITIES, ABILITY, ABLE, ABLES and ABUSE.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (219), -ED (214), -NG (212).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 548 of these words.
  • The average length is 11.1 letters.
  • After DIS, the letter C comes next most often (306 words).
