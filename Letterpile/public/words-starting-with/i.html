Words Starting With I

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 6,975 words that start with I, about 4.0% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from ID, IF and IN at the short end to IMMUNOELECTROPHORETICALLY at the long end. The highest base score belongs to IMMUNOCYTOCHEMICALLY (41 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 694. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 58 still form a word when the leading I is removed, for example IBICES, IBIS, IBISES, ICON and ICONES → BICES, BIS, BISES, CON and CONES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,151), -LY (530), -ED (459).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 1,905 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.7 letters.
  • After I, the letter N comes next most often (4,510 words).
