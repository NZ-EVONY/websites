Words Starting With HY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 993 entries are words that start with HY. That is about 0.58% of the list. They run from HYP at the short end to HYPERCHOLESTEROLEMIAS and HYPERSUSCEPTIBILITIES at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is HYPOPHYSECTOMIZING (48 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 160 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 30 still form a word when the leading HY is removed, for example HYALINE, HYALINES, HYBRIS, HYDROPS and HYDROPSIES → ALINE, ALINES, BRIS, DROPS and DROPSIES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (156), -IC (95), -AS (47).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 305 of these words.
  • The average length is 11.6 letters.
  • After HY, the letter P comes next most often (598 words).
