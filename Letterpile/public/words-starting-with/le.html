Words Starting With LE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 896 words that start with LE in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.52% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 3 letters (LEA, LED and LEE) to 18 (LEPIDOPTEROLOGICAL and LEPIDOPTEROLOGISTS). The highest base score belongs to LEXICOGRAPHICALLY (37 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 200. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • The most common endings here are -ES (119), -ED (55), -NG (55).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 262 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.4 letters.
  • After LE, the letter A comes next most often (195 words).
