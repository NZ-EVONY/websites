Words Starting With R

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 10,145 entries are words that start with R. That is about 5.9% of the list. The longest is REINSTITUTIONALIZATIONS; the shortest is RE. By common tile values the top scorer is RAZZAMATAZZES (51 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 1,582 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 8 read the same backward and forward, such as RADAR, REDDER and REFER.
  • 397 still form a word when the leading R is removed, for example RACE, RACED, RACES, RACING and RAD → ACE, ACED, ACES, ACING and AD.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,269), -NG (1,242), -ED (1,189).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 3,298 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • After R, the letter E comes next most often (6,674 words).
