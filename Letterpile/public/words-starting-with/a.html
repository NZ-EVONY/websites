Words Starting With A

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 10,444 entries are words that start with A. That is about 6.1% of the list. They run from AA, AB and AD at the short end to ACETYLCHOLINESTERASES, ADRENOCORTICOSTEROIDS and ADRENOCORTICOTROPHINS at the long end. The highest base score belongs to ANTHROPOMORPHIZATIONS (42 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 1,603. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • 12 read the same backward and forward, such as AA, ABA and ABBA.
  • 746 still form a word when the leading A is removed, for example AAH, AAL, AALS, AAS and ABA → AH, AL, ALS, AS and BA.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,503), -ED (511), -NG (507).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 3,149 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.3 letters.
  • After A, the letter N comes next most often (1,960 words).
