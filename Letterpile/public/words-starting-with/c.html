Words Starting With C

Last updated: October 1, 2026

16,207 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with C (about 9.4%). The longest is CARBOXYMETHYLCELLULOSES; the shortest are CAB, CAD and CAM. The highest base score belongs to CARBOXYMETHYLCELLULOSES (47 points), using commonly used tile values. 3,263 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as CIVIC.
  • 980 still form a word when the leading C is removed, for example CAB, CABLE, CABLES, CABS and CACHE → AB, ABLE, ABLES, ABS and ACHE.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (2,229), -ED (1,135), -NG (1,095).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 5,075 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.4 letters.
  • After C, the letter O comes next most often (6,419 words).
