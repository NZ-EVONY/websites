Words Starting With PL

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,000 entries are words that start with PL. That is about 0.58% of the list. The longest is PLETHYSMOGRAPHICALLY; the shortest is PLY. By common tile values the top scorer is PLETHYSMOGRAPHICALLY (41 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 319 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 329 of these words.

  • After PL, the letter A comes next most often (529 words).
