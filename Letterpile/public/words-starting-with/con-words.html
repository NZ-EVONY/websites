Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 1,946 words that start with CON, about 1.1% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is CONSTITUTIONALIZATIONS; the shortest is CON. The highest base score belongs to CONTEXTUALIZING (34 points), using commonly used tile values. 137 have no repeated letter at all.
