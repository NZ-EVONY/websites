Words Starting With TY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 178 entries are words that start with TY. That is about 0.10% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (TYE) to 16 (TYRANNICALNESSES). By common tile values the top scorer is TYPOGRAPHICALLY (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 50 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 23 still form a word when the leading TY is removed, for example TYER, TYERS, TYES, TYNE and TYPAL → ER, ERS, ES, NE and PAL.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (31), -NG (13), -RS (9).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 59 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • After TY, the letter P comes next most often (93 words).
