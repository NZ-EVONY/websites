Words Starting With COR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 557 words that start with COR, about 0.32% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from COR at the short end to CORRESPONDENCIES and CORRUPTIBILITIES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to CORYNEBACTERIUM (26 points), using commonly used tile values. 117 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
