Words Starting With GU

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 526 words that start with GU, about 0.31% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 3 letters (GUL, GUM and GUN) to 15 (GUILELESSNESSES and GUILTLESSNESSES). The highest base score belongs to GUZZLING (28 points), using commonly used tile values. 157 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 29 still form a word when the leading GU is removed, for example GUAN, GUAR, GUARS, GUAVA and GUDE → AN, AR, ARS, AVA and DE.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (65), -NG (37), -ED (36).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 191 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.7 letters.
  • After GU, the letter I comes next most often (88 words).
