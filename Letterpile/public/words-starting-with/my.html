Words Starting With MY

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 289 words that start with MY in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.17% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 2 letters (MY) to 18 (MYELOPROLIFERATIVE). The highest base score belongs to MYTHOLOGIZING (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 60 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 17 still form a word when the leading MY is removed, for example MYASTHENIA, MYASTHENIAS, MYASTHENIC, MYASTHENICS and MYCOSES → ASTHENIA, ASTHENIAS, ASTHENIC, ASTHENICS and COSES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (44), -IC (24), -AS (16).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 105 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.3 letters.
  • After MY, the letter O comes next most often (63 words).
