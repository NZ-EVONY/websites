Words Starting With GI

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 347 entries are words that start with GI. That is about 0.20% of the list. They run from GIB, GID and GIE at the short end to GINGERLINESSES and GINGIVECTOMIES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, GIZZARDS (28 points) scores the most. 98 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as GIG.
  • 16 still form a word when the leading GI is removed, for example GIANT, GIANTLIKE, GIANTS, GIBE and GIBED → ANT, ANTLIKE, ANTS, BE and BED.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (38), -NG (30), -ED (29).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 154 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.3 letters.
  • After GI, the letter N comes next most often (64 words).
