Words Starting With FE

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 773 entries are words that start with FE. That is about 0.45% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (FED, FEE and FEH) to 18 (FEEBLEMINDEDNESSES and FERROELECTRICITIES). The highest base score belongs to FEATHERSTITCHING (28 points), using commonly used tile values. 130 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
