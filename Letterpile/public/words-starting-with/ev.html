Words Starting With EV

Last updated: October 1, 2026

243 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with EV (about 0.14%). Lengths range from 3 letters (EVE) to 19 (EVAPOTRANSPIRATIONS). The highest base score belongs to EVANGELIZATIONS (28 points), using commonly used tile values. 12 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as EVE.
  • 17 still form a word when the leading EV is removed, for example EVACUATE, EVANGEL, EVANGELIC, EVANGELICAL and EVANGELICALLY → ACUATE, ANGEL, ANGELIC, ANGELICAL and ANGELICALLY.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (33), -NG (23), -ED (18).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 76 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • After EV, the letter A comes next most often (78 words).
