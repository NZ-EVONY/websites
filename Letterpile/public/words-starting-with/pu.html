Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 822 words that start with PU, about 0.48% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from PUB, PUD and PUG at the short end to PUZZLEHEADEDNESSES at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is PUZZLEHEADEDNESSES (43 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 241 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate.
