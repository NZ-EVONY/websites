Words Starting With HI

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 495 entries are words that start with HI. That is about 0.29% of the list. The longest is HISTOCOMPATIBILITIES; the shortest is HI. By common tile values the top scorer is HIEROGLYPHICALLY (33 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 92 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 26 still form a word when the leading HI is removed, for example HIDE, HIDING, HIDINGS, HIED and HIES → DE, DING, DINGS, ED and ES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (65), -ED (38), -NG (38).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 157 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.8 letters.
  • After HI, the letter S comes next most often (103 words).
