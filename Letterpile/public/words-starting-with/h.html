Words Starting With H

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 6,287 words that start with H in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 3.6% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is HEXAMETHYLENETETRAMINES; the shortest are HA, HE and HI. Using commonly used tile values, HYPOPHYSECTOMIZING (48 points) scores the most. 1,460 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Useful observations

  • 5 read the same backward and forward, such as HAH, HALALAH and HALLAH.
  • 292 still form a word when the leading H is removed, for example HACKEE, HACKEES, HAD, HAE and HAFT → ACKEE, ACKEES, AD, AE and AFT.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (811), -NG (389), -ED (385).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 2,043 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • After H, the letter A comes next most often (1,538 words).
