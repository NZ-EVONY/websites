Words Starting With B

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 9,233 entries are words that start with B. That is about 5.4% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (BA, BE and BI) to 21 (BUCKMINSTERFULLERENES). By common tile values the top scorer is BENZODIAZEPINES (38 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 2,578 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • 3 read the same backward and forward, such as BIB, BOB and BUB.
  • 768 still form a word when the leading B is removed, for example BAA, BAAL, BAALS, BAAS and BABA → AA, AAL, AALS, AAS and ABA.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,107), -ED (781), -NG (766).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 3,356 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.3 letters.
  • After B, the letter A comes next most often (1,793 words).
