Words Starting With OC

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 211 words that start with OC in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.12% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 3 letters (OCA) to 17 (OCEANOGRAPHICALLY). By common tile values the top scorer is OCCIDENTALIZING (30 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 41 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 27 still form a word when the leading OC is removed, for example OCAS, OCCULT, OCCULTISM, OCCULTISMS and OCCULTIST → AS, CULT, CULTISM, CULTISMS and CULTIST.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (20), -TS (16), -AL (12).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 82 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.7 letters.
  • After OC, the letter T comes next most often (75 words).
