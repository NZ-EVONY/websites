Words Starting With J

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,367 entries are words that start with J. That is about 0.79% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (JO) to 17 (JURISPRUDENTIALLY). By common tile values the top scorer is JAZZLIKE (37 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 541 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 125 still form a word when the leading J is removed, for example JAB, JABS, JAG, JAGER and JAGERS → AB, ABS, AG, AGER and AGERS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (172), -ED (114), -NG (108).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 511 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.5 letters.
  • After J, the letter A comes next most often (395 words).
