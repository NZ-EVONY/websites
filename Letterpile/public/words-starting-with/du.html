Words Starting With DU

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 502 words that start with DU in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.29% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from DUB, DUD and DUE at the short end to DUMBFOUNDERING at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is DUCKWALKING (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 203. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as DUD.
  • 63 still form a word when the leading DU is removed, for example DUAD, DUADS, DUAL, DUALIST and DUALLY → AD, ADS, AL, ALIST and ALLY.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (61), -NG (35), -ED (33).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 199 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.3 letters.
  • After DU, the letter M comes next most often (62 words).
