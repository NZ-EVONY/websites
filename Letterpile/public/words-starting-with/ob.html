Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 394 words that start with OB, about 0.23% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest is OBJECTIONABLENESSES; the shortest are OBE and OBI. Using commonly used tile values, OBJECTIFICATIONS (32 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 90. What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 73 still form a word when the leading OB is removed, for example OBCONIC, OBCORDATE, OBES, OBEYED and OBEYER → CONIC, CORDATE, ES, EYED and EYER.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (66), -LY (33), -ED (26).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 136 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.6 letters.
  • After OB, the letter S comes next most often (123 words).
