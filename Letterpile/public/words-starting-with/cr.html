Words Starting With CR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,378 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with CR (about 0.80%). The longest is CRYSTALLOGRAPHICALLY; the shortest is CRY. By common tile values the top scorer is CRYSTALLOGRAPHICALLY (36 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 320 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
