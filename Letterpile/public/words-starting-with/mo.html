Words Starting With MO

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,703 entries are words that start with MO. That is about 0.99% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (MO) to 18 (MONOCHROMATICITIES). The highest base score belongs to MONOCARBOXYLIC (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 413 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
