Words Starting With OR

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 511 entries are words that start with OR. That is about 0.30% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (OR) to 18 (ORGANOPHOSPHORUSES, ORTHOGONALIZATIONS and ORTHOPSYCHIATRISTS). By common tile values the top scorer is ORTHOGONALIZATIONS (31 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 118 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 50 still form a word when the leading OR is removed, for example ORACHE, ORACHES, ORAD, ORAL and ORALIST → ACHE, ACHES, AD, AL and ALIST.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (79), -IC (29), -LY (27).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 159 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.3 letters.
  • After OR, the letter T comes next most often (83 words).
