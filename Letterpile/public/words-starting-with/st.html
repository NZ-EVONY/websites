Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 2,595 words that start with ST in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 1.5% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest are STEREOMICROSCOPICALLY and STRAIGHTFORWARDNESSES; the shortest is STY. Using commonly used tile values, STRAIGHTJACKETING (35 points) scores the most. 443 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
