Words Starting With TA

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,150 words that start with TA in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.67% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is TACHISTOSCOPICALLY; the shortest is TA. By common tile values the top scorer is TACHYARRHYTHMIAS (35 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 324 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as TAT.
  • 81 still form a word when the leading TA is removed, for example TABARD, TABARDED, TABARDS, TABID and TABLED → BARD, BARDED, BARDS, BID and BLED.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (132), -ED (72), -NG (71).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 418 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.6 letters.
  • After TA, the letter R comes next most often (140 words).
