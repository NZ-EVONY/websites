Words Starting With CU

Last updated: October 1, 2026

816 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with CU (about 0.47%). They run from CUB, CUD and CUE at the short end to CURMUDGEONLINESSES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to CUCKOOFLOWERS (27 points), using commonly used tile values. 249 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).

  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 264 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.0 letters.
  • After CU, the letter R comes next most often (273 words).
