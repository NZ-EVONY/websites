Words Starting With MIS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1,235 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with MIS (about 0.72%). Lengths range from 4 letters (MISE, MISO and MISS) to 20 (MISCHARACTERIZATIONS). By common tile values the top scorer is MISCHARACTERIZATIONS (38 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 133 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

About the prefix MIS-

The prefix mis means “wrongly” or “badly”: to misspell is to spell something wrongly, to misread is to read it wrongly, and a mistrial is a trial that went wrong. Unlike un- and dis-, mis- rarely means a simple opposite; it describes doing the same thing, just poorly.

That makes mis- one of the more predictable prefixes. Many verbs accept it, and the resulting words keep the spelling of the base word intact, so the base usually survives when the three letters are removed. When a word such as misspell has a double S, that is simply the prefix's S meeting a base word that also starts with S. Words like mist and miser are reminders that the spelling alone isn't enough: they start with M-I-S but have nothing to do with the prefix.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 1,097 still form a word when the leading MIS is removed, for example MISACT, MISACTED, MISACTING, MISACTS and MISADAPT → ACT, ACTED, ACTING, ACTS and ADAPT.
  • The most common endings here are -NG (222), -ED (192), -ES (152).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 461 of these words.
  • The average length is 10.0 letters.
  • After MIS, the letter C comes next most often (163 words).
