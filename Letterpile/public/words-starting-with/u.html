Words Starting With U

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 5,070 entries are words that start with U. That is about 2.9% of the list. The longest is UNREPRESENTATIVENESSES; the shortest are UH, UM and UN. By common tile values the top scorer is UNCOMMERCIALIZED (34 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 997 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as ULU.
  • 68 still form a word when the leading U is removed, for example UDO, UDOS, ULAMA, ULAMAS and UMM → DO, DOS, LAMA, LAMAS and MM.
  • The most common endings here are -ED (1,162), -NG (497), -ES (493).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 1,582 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.8 letters.
  • After U, the letter N comes next most often (3,856 words).
