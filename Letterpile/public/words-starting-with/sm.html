Words Starting With SM

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 299 words that start with SM in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.17% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from SMEW, SMIT and SMOG at the short end to SMALLHOLDINGS at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, SMOKEJACKS (29 points) scores the most. 76 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 33 still form a word when the leading SM is removed, for example SMALL, SMALLS, SMALT, SMALTO and SMALTOS → ALL, ALLS, ALT, ALTO and ALTOS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (39), -ER (29), -NG (27).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 131 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.9 letters.
  • After SM, the letter A comes next most often (86 words).
