Words Starting With RO

Last updated: October 1, 2026

856 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with RO (about 0.50%). The longest is ROENTGENOGRAPHICALLY; the shortest are ROB, ROC and ROD. The highest base score belongs to ROENTGENOGRAPHICALLY (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 258 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
