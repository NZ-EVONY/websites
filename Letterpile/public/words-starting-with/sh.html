Words Starting With SH

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 1,449 words that start with SH in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.84% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from SH at the short end to SHORTSIGHTEDNESSES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to SHOWBIZZY (38 points), using commonly used tile values. 350 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.
