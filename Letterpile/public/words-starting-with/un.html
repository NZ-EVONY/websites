Words Starting With UN

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 3,856 words that start with UN in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 2.2% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is UNREPRESENTATIVENESSES; the shortest is UN. Using commonly used tile values, UNCOMMERCIALIZED (34 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 606. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

About the prefix UN-

The prefix un does two different jobs in English, and it helps to keep them apart. Added to an adjective or participle it means “not”: unhappy, unkind, unseen. Added to a verb it reverses an action that was done earlier: you untie a knot you tied and unlock a door you locked. Because both kinds are so productive, word lists contain long runs of un- words that are easy to overlook.

For word games that matters in two ways. First, many long words are simply a shorter word with un in front, so spotting a base word on your rack plus a spare U and N can turn a modest play into a long one. Second, not every word that begins with these two letters is an un- word at all: under, uncle and unit start with the same letters but carry no prefix. The figures below count every word in the list that starts with U-N, whatever its history, and the observations show how many of them still leave a word when the two letters are removed.
