  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).

  • The most common endings here are -NG (132), -ED (112), -ES (109).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 372 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • After RES, the letter T comes next most often (160 words).
