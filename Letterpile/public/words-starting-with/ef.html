Words Starting With EF

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 129 entries are words that start with EF. That is about 0.075% of the list. They run from EF at the short end to EFFICACIOUSNESSES at the long end. Using commonly used tile values, EFFERVESCENTLY (28 points) scores the most. 4 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 28 still form a word when the leading EF is removed, for example EFFABLE, EFFACE, EFFACEABLE, EFFACED and EFFACER → FABLE, FACE, FACEABLE, FACED and FACER.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (28), -LY (11), -SS (8).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 42 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.8 letters.
  • After EF, the letter F comes next most often (123 words).
