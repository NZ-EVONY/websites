Words Starting With AV

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 187 words that start with AV in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.11% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from AVA, AVE and AVO at the short end to AVARICIOUSNESSES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to AVIANIZING (23 points), using commonly used tile values. 45 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as AVA.
  • 31 still form a word when the leading AV is removed, for example AVAIL, AVAILED, AVAILING, AVAILS and AVANT → AIL, AILED, AILING, AILS and ANT.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (33), -ED (12), -NG (12).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 64 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.3 letters.
  • After AV, the letter I comes next most often (50 words).
