Words Starting With O

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 5,840 entries are words that start with O. That is about 3.4% of the list. The longest is OVERINTELLECTUALIZATIONS; the shortest are OD, OE and OF. The highest base score belongs to OVERCOMMERCIALIZATIONS (42 points), using commonly used tile values. 1,132 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

What stands out

  • 3 read the same backward and forward, such as OHO, OTTO and OXO.
  • 177 still form a word when the leading O is removed, for example OAR, OARS, OAT, OAVES and OBE → AR, ARS, AT, AVES and BE.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (749), -NG (711), -ED (641).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 2,168 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • After O, the letter V comes next most often (1,763 words).
