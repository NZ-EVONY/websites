Words Starting With OF

Last updated: October 1, 2026

119 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with OF (about 0.069%). They run from OF at the short end to OFFENSIVENESSES, OFFHANDEDNESSES and OFFICIOUSNESSES at the long end. The highest base score belongs to OFFHANDEDNESSES (26 points), using commonly used tile values. 6 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 17 still form a word when the leading OF is removed, for example OFAY, OFAYS, OFFED, OFFENCE and OFFENCES → AY, AYS, FED, FENCE and FENCES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (16), -NG (10), -ED (8).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 41 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
