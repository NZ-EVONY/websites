Words Starting With M

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 9,616 entries are words that start with M. That is about 5.6% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (MA, ME and MI) to 24 (MICROELECTROPHORETICALLY and MICROSPECTROPHOTOMETRIES). The highest base score belongs to MICROELECTROPHORETICALLY (40 points), using commonly used tile values. 1,894 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • 8 read the same backward and forward, such as MADAM, MARRAM and MEM.
  • 348 still form a word when the leading M is removed, for example MACE, MACED, MACERATE, MACERATED and MACES → ACE, ACED, ACERATE, ACERATED and ACES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,328), -ED (637), -NG (620).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 2,953 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.2 letters.
  • After M, the letter I comes next most often (2,627 words).
