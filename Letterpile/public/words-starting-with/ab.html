Words Starting With AB

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 622 entries are words that start with AB. That is about 0.36% of the list. The longest is ABSENTMINDEDNESSES; the shortest is AB. By common tile values the top scorer is ABSQUATULATING (26 points); board bonuses aren't counted. Words with no repeated letter: 126. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • 2 read the same backward and forward, such as ABA and ABBA.
  • 153 still form a word when the leading AB is removed, for example ABAFT, ABALONE, ABAMP, ABAMPERE and ABAMPERES → AFT, ALONE, AMP, AMPERE and AMPERES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (80), -ED (42), -NG (41).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 242 of these words.
  • The average length is 8.6 letters.
  • After AB, the letter S comes next most often (174 words).
