Words Starting With ANTI

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 785 words that start with ANTI in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.46% of the words Letterpile uses. They run from ANTIC and ANTIS at the short end to ANTIAUTHORITARIANISMS and ANTIFERROMAGNETICALLY at the long end. The highest base score belongs to ANTISCHIZOPHRENIC (38 points), using commonly used tile values. 37 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

About the prefix ANTI-

The prefix anti means “against” or “opposite to”: antiwar, antifreeze, antibody. It comes from Greek, and English uses it freely to build new words, many of them in science, medicine and politics. Like non-, it is often written with a hyphen in everyday writing, but this page counts only words that the ENABLE list spells solid.

A few words start with A-N-T-I without containing the prefix in its “against” sense. antic and antique are examples, and some words that begin ante mean “before”, a different prefix with a similar spelling. The “still form a word” count below is a spelling check and will include some of these coincidences.

Because anti- is four letters with only two vowels, anti- words tend to be long and consonant-rich. They rarely matter in everyday tile play, but they are useful for crossword-style puzzles where the length and a few crossing letters are known.
