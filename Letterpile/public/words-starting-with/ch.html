Words Starting With CH

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 2,180 words that start with CH, about 1.3% of the 172,434 words this site shows. The longest are CHEMOTHERAPEUTICALLY and CHLOROFLUOROMETHANES; the shortest is CHI. By common tile values the top scorer is CHLORDIAZEPOXIDES (42 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 548 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.
