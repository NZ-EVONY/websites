Words Starting With PS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 327 entries are words that start with PS. That is about 0.19% of the list. The longest are PSEUDOCHOLINESTERASES, PSEUDOSOPHISTICATIONS and PSYCHOPHARMACOLOGICAL; the shortest is PSI. By common tile values the top scorer is PSYCHOPHYSIOLOGICALLY (45 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 28 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 8 still form a word when the leading PS is removed, for example PSALMS, PSALTER, PSALTERS, PSHAW and PSHAWED → ALMS, ALTER, ALTERS, HAW and HAWED.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (48), -IC (36), -LY (19).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 81 of these words.
  • The average length is 12.5 letters.
  • After PS, the letter Y comes next most often (190 words).
