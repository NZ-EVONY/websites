Words Starting With E

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 7,057 entries are words that start with E. That is about 4.1% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (ED, EF and EH) to 28 (ETHYLENEDIAMINETETRAACETATES). The highest base score belongs to ELECTROENCEPHALOGRAPHICALLY (47 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 787. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • 6 read the same backward and forward, such as EKE, EME and ERE.
  • 331 still form a word when the leading E is removed, for example EAGER, EAGERS, EAGLET, EAGLETS and EAR → AGER, AGERS, AGLET, AGLETS and AR.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (1,022), -NG (505), -ED (497).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 4, found in 2,080 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.5 letters.
  • After E, the letter X comes next most often (1,475 words).
