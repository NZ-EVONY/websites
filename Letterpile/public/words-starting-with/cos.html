Words Starting With COS

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 159 words that start with COS, about 0.092% of the 172,434 words this site shows. They run from COS at the short end to COSMOCHEMISTRIES and COSMOPOLITANISMS at the long end. The highest base score belongs to COSMETICIZING (29 points), using commonly used tile values. 40 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 21 still form a word when the leading COS is removed, for example COSES, COSET, COSHER, COSHERS and COSHES → ES, ET, HER, HERS and HES.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (20), -TS (12), -ED (11).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 47 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.2 letters.
  • After COS, the letter M comes next most often (58 words).
