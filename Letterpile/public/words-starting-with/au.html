Words Starting With AU

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 542 words that start with AU in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.31% of the words Letterpile uses. The longest is AUTHORITATIVENESSES; the shortest is AUK. The highest base score belongs to AUTOBIOGRAPHICALLY (31 points), using commonly used tile values. Words with no repeated letter: 100. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 26 still form a word when the leading AU is removed, for example AUBADE, AUBURN, AUBURNS, AUDAD and AUDADS → BADE, BURN, BURNS, DAD and DADS.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (86), -IC (28), -NG (28).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 5, found in 146 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.9 letters.
  • After AU, the letter T comes next most often (306 words).
