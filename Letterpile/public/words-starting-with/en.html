Words Starting With EN

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 1,433 entries are words that start with EN. That is about 0.83% of the list. Lengths range from 2 letters (EN) to 22 (ENCEPHALOMYOCARDITISES). The highest base score belongs to ENCEPHALOMYOCARDITISES (37 points), using commonly used tile values. 126 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 580 still form a word when the leading EN is removed, for example ENABLE, ENABLER, ENABLES, ENACT and ENACTED → ABLE, ABLER, ABLES, ACT and ACTED.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (196), -NG (153), -ED (151).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 587 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.4 letters.
  • After EN, the letter D comes next most often (273 words).
