Words Starting With SK

Last updated: October 1, 2026

There are 404 words that start with SK in the ENABLE list, which is roughly 0.23% of the words Letterpile uses. Lengths range from 3 letters (SKA, SKI and SKY) to 14 (SKATEBOARDINGS, SKILLESSNESSES and SKILLFULNESSES). By common tile values the top scorer is SKYJACKINGS (32 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 83 have no repeated letter at all. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 40 still form a word when the leading SK is removed, for example SKAG, SKAS, SKAT, SKATE and SKATES → AG, AS, AT, ATE and ATES.
  • The most common endings here are -NG (47), -ED (44), -ES (34).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 196 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.8 letters.
  • After SK, the letter I comes next most often (181 words).
