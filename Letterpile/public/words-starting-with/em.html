Words Starting With EM

Last updated: October 1, 2026

610 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with EM (about 0.35%). They run from EM at the short end to EMBOURGEOISEMENTS and EMOTIONLESSNESSES at the long end. By common tile values the top scorer is EMBEZZLEMENTS (37 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 82 have no repeated letter at all. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

What stands out

  • 1 reads the same backward and forward, such as EME.
  • 185 still form a word when the leading EM is removed, for example EMBALM, EMBALMS, EMBANK, EMBANKED and EMBANKING → BALM, BALMS, BANK, BANKED and BANKING.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (82), -ED (65), -NG (62).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 225 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.1 letters.
  • After EM, the letter B comes next most often (262 words).
