Words Starting With Z

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The open ENABLE word list has 532 words that start with Z, about 0.31% of the 172,434 words this site shows. Lengths range from 3 letters (ZAG, ZAP and ZAX) to 17 (ZOOGEOGRAPHICALLY). Using commonly used tile values, ZYZZYVAS (44 points) scores the most. Words with no repeated letter: 186. Games use their own dictionaries, so a word here isn't automatically playable everywhere.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 41 still form a word when the leading Z is removed, for example ZAG, ZAMIA, ZAMIAS, ZANY and ZARF → AG, AMIA, AMIAS, ANY and ARF.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (62), -AS (25), -ED (20).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 168 of these words.
  • The average length is 7.4 letters.
  • After Z, the letter O comes next most often (165 words).
