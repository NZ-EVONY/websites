Useful observations

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 17 still form a word when the leading LY is removed, for example LYART, LYCEE, LYCEES, LYES and LYRATE → ART, CEE, CEES, ES and RATE.
  • The most common endings here are -ES (36), -IC (12), -ED (10).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 2, found in 52 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.4 letters.
  • After LY, the letter M comes next most often (45 words).
