Words Starting With RET

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counting only the ENABLE word list, 449 entries are words that start with RET. That is about 0.26% of the list. Lengths range from 3 letters (RET) to 19 (RETICULOENDOTHELIAL). By common tile values the top scorer is RETICULOENDOTHELIAL (25 points); board bonuses aren't counted. 37 have no repeated letter at all. Different games use different dictionaries, so check your game's rules before relying on a word.

Patterns in this list

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 70 still form a word when the leading RET is removed, for example RETABLE, RETABLES, RETAG, RETAIL and RETAILED → ABLE, ABLES, AG, AIL and AILED.
  • The most common endings here are -NG (61), -ED (56), -ES (54).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 172 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.4 letters.
  • After RET, the letter R comes next most often (171 words).
