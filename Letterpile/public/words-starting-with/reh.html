Words Starting With REH

Last updated: October 1, 2026

100 of the 172,434 ENABLE words shown on this site are words that start with REH (about 0.058%). The longest is REHOSPITALIZATIONS; the shortest are REHAB and REHEM. The highest base score belongs to REHOSPITALIZATIONS (32 points), using commonly used tile values. 6 of them use no letter twice, which matters in games where every tile is separate. This is a general word list, not any game's official one; always check the rules of your game.

What stands out

  • None of these words is a palindrome (the same backward and forward).
  • 23 still form a word when the leading REH is removed, for example REHAB, REHABS, REHASH, REHASHED and REHASHES → AB, ABS, ASH, ASHED and ASHES.
  • The most common endings here are -NG (21), -ED (18), -ES (11).
  • The most common number of vowels (A, E, I, O, U) is 3, found in 38 of these words.
  • The average length is 9.6 letters.
  • After REH, the letter A comes next most often (37 words).
