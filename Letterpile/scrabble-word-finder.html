Reading the results

Each tile shows the word's base score from standard Scrabble letter values. Blank tiles score zero, so a word that needs a blank is worth less than the same word from real tiles; the finder always spends your real tiles first. Words that use all seven of your tiles get the 50-point bingo bonus and a coloured outline.

Using board letters

Type a letter or run of letters that's already on the board, such as E or ING, and the finder only shows words that pass through it. Those board tiles are counted in the score but don't come from your rack.

Strategy notes

  • Two-letter words win games. QI, ZA, XU, JO and AX let you park a big tile on a premium square with parallel plays.
  • Keep a balanced leave. After you play, the tiles you keep matter. A leave like ERS or AEST sets up bingos; five consonants doesn't.
  • Hold your S and blanks for a play that earns at least 8–10 extra points with them.
