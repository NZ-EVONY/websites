Anagram Solver

An anagram uses every letter exactly once. Enter a word, name or short phrase to find single words and two-word phrases made from all of it.

Anagrams vs. words within

This page only shows arrangements that use all your letters: LISTEN gives SILENT, TINSEL and ENLIST. For shorter words made from some of the letters, use the Word Unscrambler.

Two-word anagrams

With two-word anagrams on, the solver splits your letters into two real words of at least three letters each. That's how DORMITORY becomes DIRTY ROOM. Long inputs produce hundreds of pairs, so the list stops at 400.

Where anagrams show up

Cryptic crossword clues, the Jumble, puzzle hunts, pen names (plenty of authors have hidden their real names in anagrams), and wedding-reception games.

Tip

Spaces and punctuation are ignored, so you can paste a full name like “Clint Eastwood” and see what it rearranges into.
