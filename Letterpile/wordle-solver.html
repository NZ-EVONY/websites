How the solver thinks

  • Green pins a letter to that spot.
  • Yellow means the letter is in the word, just not there.
  • Grey means there are no more copies of that letter than the green and yellow ones you've already found. That's how double letters work: guess SPEED for a word with one E, and the second E turns grey without ruling E out.

Choosing your next guess

Suggestions are ranked by how common their letters are among the words still possible. A guess made of widely shared letters splits the field hardest, whatever colours come back. Words with repeated letters rank lower because they test fewer letters.

Our list is a full English dictionary, so it includes obscure words the game won't pick as answers. If the top suggestion looks odd, the next few usually include the everyday word.

Tip

Good openers use five different common letters. SLATE, CRANE and TRACE are popular choices.
