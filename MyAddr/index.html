Questions

        
What is an IP address?
          
A numeric label assigned to your connection so that traffic can find its way back to you. Your router has one public address shared by every device in the house; each device also has a private address inside your network, which websites never see.

        
Why do I have two addresses?
          
IPv4 is the original 32-bit scheme and ran out of room years ago. IPv6 is its 128-bit successor. Many connections carry both. If the IPv6 box says "Not available", your network or ISP simply hasn't enabled it.

        
Can someone find my home from my IP?
          
Not from the IP alone. Public geolocation stops at the city or region, and often points to your ISP's nearest exchange. Only your ISP can link an address to a subscriber.

        
How do I change my IP?
          
Restarting your router sometimes gets a new one from your ISP. A VPN or proxy replaces it, as far as websites are concerned, with the address of the server you connect through.
